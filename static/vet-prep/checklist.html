Before vaccination and testing

  • Official destination rules opened (country or route page on petpolicycheck.com/vet-prep/)
  • ISO-compatible microchip implanted and readable; number matches every document
  • For Japan (non-designated regions): chip placed before the first rabies vaccine

Rabies vaccination

  • Rabies vaccine recorded (product, batch, dates of vaccination and validity)
  • EU or Great Britain: vaccine at 12 weeks or older, then 21 days after the primary course
  • Japan (non-designated regions): first vaccine at 91 days or older; second at least 30 days later

Rabies titer, if required

  • EU unlisted country (South Korea is not listed): sample at least 30 days after primary vaccination and not less than 90 days before the certificate
  • Great Britain unlisted country: titer, then 3 months after the blood sample
  • Japan (non-designated regions): result at least 0.5 IU/ml, then 180 days from the blood draw
  • South Korea, age 90 days or older from a non-rabies-free origin: titer at least 0.5 IU/ml from an approved lab

Certificate and endorsement

  • Correct destination certificate prepared (VEHCS in the United States)
  • Clinical examination inside the validity window (EU and Great Britain: 10 days; Japan: exam within 10 days before boarding)
  • APHIS endorsement completed when required (ink for UK, France, Germany, Korea; digital for Japan)
  • Mexico dogs from the US: screwworm inspection certificate within 5 days; no APHIS endorsement
  • Original or printed endorsed certificate packed to travel with the pet

Entry to the United States and other extras

  • CDC Dog Import Form receipt for dogs entering the United States (age 6 months or older, microchip, healthy appearance)
  • High-risk-country documents, if they apply; US-issued rabies certification endorsed before the dog leaves the United States
  • Great Britain: dog tapeworm treatment 24 to 120 hours before arrival, unless an official exemption applies
  • EU: dogs entering Finland, Ireland, Malta, Northern Ireland or Norway need veterinarian-administered tapeworm treatment 24 to 120 hours before scheduled entry
  • Japan: AQS notified at least 40 days ahead
  • Airline told whether the pet travels in the cabin or as cargo

Information only, not veterinary or legal advice. Confirm every step with the destination authority and with APHIS, CFIA or the equivalent official body.
