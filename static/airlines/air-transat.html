Air Transat pet policy

✓ Last verified Nov 18, 2026 against the official Air Transat pets and assistance dogs page.

Snub-nosed / brachycephalic breeds

Compare official cabin and hold/cargo rules for short-nosed dogs and cats across 12 airlines: Snub-nosed breeds airline rules (Official rule).

Pets in cabinYes
Species acceptedDomestic cats and dogs only. All other animals are not accepted on Air Transat flights.
Max carrier sizeCabin: soft-sided only, must not exceed 25 cm (10 in) in height × 24 cm (9.5 in) in width × 43 cm (17 in) in length, and must fit under the seat. Hard-sided carriers are not permitted in the cabin.
Max weight (pet + carrier)Cabin: must not exceed 8 kg. Hold: maximum 45 kg (99 lb) including the carrier.
Fees
  • Cabin, per pet, per flight segment (paid up to 24 hours before departure, as published on Air Transat's Canada site): $50 to the USA or within Canada; $100 to Europe and Africa, or South.
  • Hold, per pet, per flight segment (paid up to 24 hours before departure, as published on the same page): $100 within Canada; $150 to the USA; $275 to Europe and Africa, or South.
  • Upon entry into Canada, a CAD $30 fee (plus tax, per animal) is required for inspection by the Canadian Food Inspection Agency; arrivals from the USA are exempt.
Checked bag / cargo optionYes: cats and dogs in the checked baggage hold, in a plain rigid leak-proof plastic cage (wire doors are acceptable; cages with a top petting door are not). Maximum carrier size at check-in: 122 cm × 81.3 cm × 89 cm (48 × 32 × 35 in). Larger cages must go via Air Transat cargo, with advance arrangements. Hold space is not available on connecting or codeshare flights, flights operated by another carrier, vacation packages, or Train + Air.
Breed restrictionsAir Transat will not transport the following breeds or mixed breeds, including one or more of these: mastiffs (all breeds, including Dogo Argentino, Bandog, Boerboel, Fila Brasileiro, Cane Corso, Neapolitan, Presa Canario and Tosa Inu); pit bulls (all breeds, including American Bully, American Pit Bull Terrier, American Staffordshire Terrier and Staffordshire Bull Terrier); Akita; Caucasian Ovcharka; Kangal/Anatolian Shepherd (Karabash); Rottweiler; wolf-dog hybrids; and Doberman Pinscher. Bull Mastiffs and French Mastiffs (Dogue de Bordeaux) are exempt from that list and are accepted. For the hold, the official page also lists French Bulldogs and American Bulldogs among breeds it is unable to transport, and states that French Bulldogs are allowed in the cabin only. Other brachycephalic (snub-nosed) dogs and cats not on the refusal list are accepted in the hold only with extra conditions, including a kennel one size larger than the normal standard and ventilation openings on all four sides.
Route restrictionsCabin pets are not available on codeshare flights or vacation packages. To and from the United Kingdom, cats and dogs are accepted as cargo only (certified service dogs are excepted). To the Republic of Ireland, cats and dogs are accepted as cargo only (certified service dogs excepted); from Ireland, cats and dogs may be accepted in the cabin, in the hold, or as cargo, subject to Air Transat's acceptance conditions.
Service animalsWhen accompanied by certification and documentation and travelling with a person with a disability, emotional support and certified service dogs are welcome in the passenger cabin. Service animals on duty fly in the cabin free of charge.

Good to know

Reserve cabin or hold space through the Air Transat Contact Centre no later than 24 hours before scheduled departure; pets without a reservation will not be accepted at the airport. Fees must be paid at the time of booking and are non-refundable if conditions are not met. Passengers must be at least 16 years old to travel with an animal. Complete the mandatory Air Transat Pet Information Form and present a printed or electronic copy at check-in, together with a valid health certificate stating the pet's breed and proof of rabies vaccination. Arrive with the animal at least 120 minutes (2 hours) before departure. In the cabin, only one animal per passenger is allowed; the carrier counts as the carry-on item (no additional carry-on); the passenger cannot sit in Club Class, an emergency-exit seat or a bulkhead seat; and unaccompanied minors and passengers travelling with medical equipment cannot bring a cabin pet. Minimum age: 12 weeks and fully weaned, except flights to the United States (at least 6 months) and Guyana (at least 4 months). In the hold, only one animal per cage is accepted, and no animal under 12 weeks may travel.

Before you book, re-check the official policy official page and confirm the current rules, space and fees with the airline; space is usually limited per flight.
