Pet owners and assistance dog users travelling with dogs, cats or ferrets between Great Britain and the European Union.

At the second EU–UK Summit in Brussels on 20 November 2026, EU and UK leaders welcomed the conclusion of negotiations on a Common Sanitary and Phytosanitary (SPS) Area and stated in the [UK–EU Summit 2026 Joint Statement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ukeu-summit-2026-key-documents/uk-eu-summit-2026-joint-statement-html) (also published as EU Council document [ST 16174/26](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-16174-2026-INIT/en/pdf)): “We aim to apply this agreement from 1 March 2028.” Both sides noted that conclusion and entry into force remain subject to their respective legal frameworks and procedures, including the European Partnership Bill and secondary legislation in the UK according to the [GOV.UK SPS Explainer](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ukeu-summit-2026-key-documents/uk-eu-summit-2026-explainer-sps-html).

According to [DEFRA guidance (“Pet animals: preparing for changes”)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/pet-animals-preparing-for-changes), published on 20 November 2026, travelling to the EU with pet dogs, assistance dogs, cats and ferrets will become easier and cheaper from 1 March 2028 because pet owners will be able to obtain a multiuse pet passport valid for EU travel, rather than getting a new Animal Health Certificate (AHC) for each trip.

DEFRA explicitly states that **it is not possible to obtain a multiuse pet passport yet**, as they cannot be used until 1 March 2028, and that further details on how to get one will be provided next year. Until then, owners and assistance dog users travelling from Great Britain to an EU country still need an Animal Health Certificate, and current entry requirements published on [GOV.UK (“Taking your pet abroad”)](https://www.gov.uk/taking-your-pet-abroad) and the [European Commission non-EU entry page](https://food.ec.europa.eu/animals/live-animal-movements/dogs-cats-and-ferrets/bringing-pet-eu-non-eu-country_en) remain unchanged.
