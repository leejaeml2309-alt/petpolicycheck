HND · Arriving in Japan

Dog or cat import timeline

From a non-designated region (for example the USA, South Korea or France). D-days count back from your arrival day in Japan. D-210 is the shortest path the official intervals allow (30 days between vaccines + 180-day wait): start earlier.

  1. D-210 or earlier

    1. ISO microchip

    ISO 11784/11785 chip (15 digits), implanted before the first rabies vaccine.

  2. D-210 or earlier

    2. 1st rabies vaccine

    After the microchip (same day is fine), at 91 days old or more. Inactivated or recombinant vaccine.

  3. 30+ days later

    3. 2nd rabies vaccine

    At least 30 days after the 1st, within its effective period.

  4. D-180 or earlier

    4. Blood test, approved lab

    Drawn after the 2nd vaccine (same day is fine) at a designated laboratory; titre ≥ 0.5 IU/ml; valid 2 years.

  5. 180 days

    5. 180-day wait

    Arrive at least 180 days after the blood sample (sample day = day 0).

  6. D-40 or earlier

    6. Notify AQS

    Advance notification to AQS at your arrival airport, at least 40 days before arrival (NACCS online, or e-mail).

  7. ≤ 10 days before boarding

    7. Vet check before departure

    Clinical inspection by a vet: no signs of rabies (dogs: also leptospirosis).

  8. Before departure

    8. Official certificate

    Certificate (e.g. Form AC) endorsed by the exporting country’s government authority. Original required.

  9. D0 · arrival

    9. Import inspection

    Bring your pet in its crate or carrier to AQS on arrival in Japan.

If something is missing or wrong

Pets that do not meet the requirements, or arrive with certificate errors, are detained at an AQS facility for up to 180 days or shipped back. A wait shorter than 180 days means detention to make up the shortfall. AQS suggests sending copies of your certificates in advance for a check.

Source: Animal Quarantine Service (MAFF), “Import dogs and cats into Japan from non-designated regions”, steps 1–8, read 7 November 2026 (KST). Rules can change; confirm each step with AQS. Airline and lab steps: verify with the provider.
