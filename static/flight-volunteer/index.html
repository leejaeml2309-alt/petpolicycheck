Bilingual airport cards for Korea→US/Canada (EN+KO) and Mexico→US/Canada (EN+ES). Emphasizes CFIA commercial vs personal rules and CDC Dog Import Form.

Critical compliance warnings

  • Canada (CFIA): Determine the import category from ownership, the importer and intended use, not the escort’s volunteer status alone. CFIA has personal pathways for accompanied and unaccompanied pets; dogs intended for adoption, fostering or transfer are commercial imports. Confirm the applicable conditions before booking. Canada prohibits commercial dog imports from countries on CFIA’s high-risk dog-rabies list until further notice; import permits are not issued for those imports. Check CFIA’s list, not the CDC list, before committing to transport. Confirm on official CFIA guidance before the flight.
  • Volunteers: Volunteering is not an import category or a blanket exemption. In the US, APHIS Animal Care regulates resale, including fee-based adoption after import, but lists dogs adopted abroad and delivered to their owners without further resale as outside that AWA scope. CDC, other APHIS and state requirements still apply. Canada uses its own commercial-import definitions.
  • USA (CDC): Almost every dog needs a CDC Dog Import Form receipt. High-risk vs low-risk pathways differ.
  • Mexico (SENASICA): For pet dogs travelling from the US to Mexico, follow OISA/SENASICA entry inspection and the NWS inspection-document requirement effective August 3, 2026. Dogs entering the US from Mexico require separate screwworm-freedom certification. Confirm export requirements for the actual origin and purpose of movement.
