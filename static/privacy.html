Privacy policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026

PetPolicyCheck is a mostly static website. This page explains, in plain English, what data is and isn't collected when you visit, including by the analytics service we use and, on some product pages, Amazon Associates.

Forum accounts

Reading the forum needs no account. If you create one to post or comment, we store: your email address (to sign you in and for account emails such as confirmation and password reset; never shown publicly), your display name (public, shown next to your posts), a hashed password (if you don't use Google sign-in), and what you post: posts, comments, reports, their categories and dates, and the moderation status and reason. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your email address and basic profile with us for sign-in; we don't use your Google name or photo publicly.

Processor: accounts and forum content are stored by Supabase (our database and sign-in provider), which processes the data on our behalf; our project is hosted in Supabase's Northeast Asia (Seoul) region. Supabase may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. Sign-in keeps a session token in your browser's local storage (not an ad-network cookie); signing out removes it.

Moderation: posts and comments are checked before publication by an automatic keyword filter and by a moderator (assisted by an AI tool that reads only the text you submitted). Published posts and comments, with your display name, are public and may be included in search engines.

Your choices: you can delete your own posts and comments at any time from My account, and change your display name. To delete your account and all its data, or to get a copy of it, email contact@petpolicycheck.com; we handle requests within 30 days. Your data is kept while your account exists.

Forum pages for signing in and posting don't show ads or load affiliate scripts.

  • Data collected for the forum: email address, display name, password hash (email sign-up only), posts, comments, reports, and their dates and moderation status.
