Pet travel rules for Canada: CFIA entry, hubs and return to the US

Federal rules from the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA), with border checks by CBSA. Verified 2026-11-06. Always confirm with CFIA and your airline before you fly.

Federal rules (CFIA)

Authority: Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA) — border checks by CBSA.

Dogs

Rabies
Personal dogs ≥3 months: valid rabies certificate (EN/FR, licensed vet, identifies dog). <3 months: none, proof of age.
Health certificate
Not required for personal dogs ≥8 months. Unaccompanied dogs <8 months: vet health certificate within 72h.
Microchip
Not required by CFIA for personal dogs (airline may require). Required for US return (CDC).
Permit / commercial
Personal: none. Commercial (resale, adoption, fostering/rescue, breeding, show, research): import permit; commercial dogs from CFIA high-risk dog-rabies countries PROHIBITED since 28 Sep 2022.
  • Personal vs commercial is the key split; rescue/adoption dogs = commercial.
  • Dogs <8 months unaccompanied or commercial face stricter rules (health cert ≤72h, microchip/permit for commercial).
  • High-risk list includes Dominican Republic, Brazil, Colombia, Peru… (MX & US not on it) — a DO rescue dog cannot enter Canada as commercial.
  • Inspector can order rabies vaccination at owner's expense if non-compliant.

Cats

Rabies
Cats ≥3 months: vet certificate (EN/FR) showing current rabies vaccination, OR 6-month residence in a rabies-free country, OR RNATT. <3 months: no restrictions.
Health certificate
Not required.
Microchip
Not required by CFIA.
  • Hybrid/non-domestic cats follow separate rules.

Breed alerts

No federal breed ban.

Ontario (province)

Dog Owners' Liability Act bans pit bulls (pit bull terrier, Staffordshire bull terrier, American Staffordshire terrier, American pit bull terrier & look-alikes) — no import/ownership except pre-2005 'restricted pit bulls'. Bill 147 repeal attempt never enacted.

Source

City of Winnipeg (MB)

Responsible Pet Ownership By-law: APBT, Staffordshire Bull Terrier, AmStaff (and look-alikes) cannot be brought into the city; temporary permit possible.

Source

Return to the United States

CDC (since 1 Aug 2024): every dog entering/returning to the US must be ≥6 months, microchipped, healthy, with a CDC Dog Import Form receipt. Dogs only in CA/US for 6 months need no rabies certificate; receipt reusable 6 months if departure country stays Canada. Source: https://www.cdc.gov/importation/dogs/faqs.html (HTTP 403 to curl — bot protection; content confirmed via official search snippet).

Rail & city transit

VIA Rail: Corridor (Québec City–Windsor): small cat/dog in cabin, carrier ≤55×40×27 cm, pet+carrier ≤10 kg, book ≥48h; fee ~CA$50+tax per direction (from VIA PDF via search). Select long-distance routes: baggage car (not May 1–Sep 30). Official page.

Uber Pet: Select Canadian cities; check app.

Hotel chains (verified policies)

  • Le Germain / Alt Hotels — Pets welcome subject to availability, 1 pet/room, CA$45 per stay, not left unattended ($200 penalty). Alt in YHZ (at airport), YUL, YYZ, YYC, YYT…
  • Fairmont (Canada) — Property-specific: e.g. Fairmont Hotel Vancouver CA$50/night (max $100), Château Laurier $60/stay, Château Frontenac $60/night (dogs), Hotel Macdonald $35/pet/day.
  • Kimpton (IHG) — Brand policy: pets of any size/breed, no fee/deposit (HTTP 403 to curl — confirmed via official search snippet). Canada: Kimpton Saint George Toronto — confirm property.

Grooming

Canadian salons are vaccine-strict: rabies proof is near-universal, many add DHPP + Bordetella (Montreal often adds canine flu + lepto). Vet-hospital groomers (VCA) may offer sedation; independent mobile vans (Calgary) are the best 'post-flight hotel' fit.

No Canadian salon checked states whether a US/EU rabies certificate is accepted → assume original English/French rabies certificate needed and call ahead; Bordetella-requiring salons will refuse many foreign dogs.
