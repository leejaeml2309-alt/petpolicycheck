Pet travel rules for South Korea: APQA import of dogs and cats

The Animal and Plant Quarantine Agency (APQA) sets the official rules for bringing a dog or cat into the Republic of Korea. Requirements depend on the animal’s age and on whether the country of origin is designated rabies-free. Checked 14 November 2026. Always confirm with APQA and your airline before you travel.

Every entry rule below is labelled Official rule. Nothing here is legal or veterinary advice.

Entry rules (APQA)

Authority: APQA — Bring a dog or a cat to Korea. Checked 14 Nov 2026

  • Official rule A microchip must be ISO-compliant (ISO 11784 and 11785). For any other chip, the importer must bring a scanner that can read it.
  • Official rule Cats and dogs younger than 90 days, or originating from rabies-free countries, do not require prior rabies vaccination, but they must be microchipped. The owner must submit a health certificate issued by the government of the exporting country that states the microchip number and the date of birth. APQA points to a separate list of areas designated as rabies-free (that list can change); check the current list before you travel. This guide does not name individual countries on that list.
  • Official rule For cats and dogs aged 90 days or older originating from countries that are not rabies-free, a microchip and a rabies-neutralising antibody test are mandatory. The test must be carried out by an internationally approved laboratory or by the competent authority of the exporting country, and the blood must have been collected within the previous 24 months. On arrival you must submit a government health certificate that confirms the microchip number and the antibody-test result. A titre below 0.5 IU/ml, a missing test, or a missing or mismatched microchip can lead to an extended quarantine at the owner’s expense. APQA states that the average extended quarantine is 10 days and that the period can be longer depending on the case. Without a health certificate, the animal must be returned to the exporting country at the owner’s cost.
  • Official rule If the documents are verified and health is confirmed by a clinical examination at the port of entry, the animal may be released on the day of arrival. Advance notification to APQA for the use of a quarantine facility is required when a traveller brings ten or more cats and/or dogs.

Return to the United States

Official rule Dogs entering the United States need the CDC Dog Import Form and must meet the age, microchip and health rules, plus any documents that apply if the dog has been in a high-risk country. South Korea is not named on the CDC high-risk dog-rabies list dated 15 April 2026. Confirm on the current CDC pages before you fly. Pet-friendly taxi

Official rule The official Easy Law guide (Ministry of Government Legislation) states that whether a pet may ride in a taxi is decided by the taxi operator’s conditions of carriage or business instructions, under the Passenger Transport Service Act, article 9. Ask the operator before you book. Easy Law: using public transport with a pet Checked 14 Nov 2026

Official rule The same official guide states that when a person with a disability is accompanied by an assistance dog wearing the official assistance-dog mark, the dog must be accepted on public transport; refusing that access can lead to an administrative fine (Welfare of Persons with Disabilities Act, article 40(3) and article 90(3)3). The official page does not restate a won amount in the extract opened for this guide.

Official rule Kakao T Pet is Kakao Mobility’s official dedicated pet transport service. You can book a date, time and place, or a time-hire. The official paid-service fare page (opened 14 November 2026) publishes a general-reservation fare of a KRW 8,000 base plus KRW 97 per 100 m, a demand-based multiplier of 0.5 to 2.0 times, and tolls; the fare is confirmed in the booking screen (a change of destination or an added stop can change the fare). A time-hire table is also published, starting at KRW 35,000 for 30 minutes (15 km). Kakao Mobility states that if the vehicle is soiled by the pet, Kakao T covers the cleaning cost. Confirm the current table in the app before you book. Kakao T Pet · Kakao Mobility: paid-service fares Checked 14 Nov 2026

Train

Official rule Easy Law (citing the Railroad Safety Act and Korail’s conditions): to take a pet on a train, the pet including its carrier must not occupy a seat or the aisle; the animal must travel in a dedicated bag or carrier so that it is not exposed; and it must have had the necessary vaccinations, including rabies. Boarding with a pet without those safety measures, if it causes harm to other passengers, can lead to removal from the train or an administrative fine of not more than 500,000 won. Easy Law: using public transport with a pet Checked 14 Nov 2026

Official rule Korail’s official passenger conditions (revised 18 April 2025, article 22) allow a pet animal that will not harm or inconvenience others, that has had the necessary vaccinations, and that travels in a dedicated bag (or equivalent). Other animals are excluded. The article says detailed size and weight limits are posted on the Korail website — confirm those details there; this guide does not restate a weight or length that is not in that article. The official Korail FAQ states the same carrier and vaccination rule (including rabies) and adds that if a separate seat is needed, an adult fare must be purchased. Korail passenger conditions (PDF, 18 April 2025) · Korail official FAQ on companion animals Checked 14 Nov 2026

Official rule Seoul Metro passenger conditions for lines 1–8 (revised 7 March 2026, article 35) prohibit animals in stations and on trains, with these exceptions: a small number of birds, small insects and small pet animals kept out of sight in a dedicated carrier, without an offensive odour; and an assistance dog wearing the official assistance-dog mark. Seoul Metro: passenger conditions (lines 1–8) Checked 14 Nov 2026

Official rule SR (operator of SRT), in an official notice dated 8 August 2022: you cannot book a separate seat for a pet; the animal travels as hand luggage, in a carrier, on your lap or at your feet, and must not occupy a seat or the aisle. Only a small pet no more than 60 cm in length is accepted, and the combined weight of the animal and the carrier must not exceed 10 kg. Necessary vaccinations, including rabies, are required. Assistance dogs for people with visual, hearing or physical disabilities may ride without a carrier. Fighting-dog types named on that notice (Tosa, Doberman, Shepherd and pit bull), birds of prey, rodents, reptiles, poultry and livestock are not accepted. Confirm that the notice still reflects the current conditions of carriage before you travel. SR notice on pets (8 August 2022)

Flights

Official rule Asiana Airlines (official English page, checked 14 November 2026): each adult passenger may bring one pet into the cabin and two pets as checked baggage; children may not travel with a cabin pet. For the cabin, the combined weight of the pet and its container must not exceed 7 kg, and the container must not exceed 21 cm in height, 32 cm in width and 45 cm in length, and must fit under the seat. For checked baggage, the pet plus cage must weigh no more than 45 kg, the three sides of the cage must total no more than 285 cm, and the height must not exceed 84 cm (some countries apply a lower weight limit, including 32 kg). Pets are not included in the free baggage allowance; a separate fee applies. Published fees include, on ICN–US routes, KRW 200,000 (USD 200) up to 32 kg including the cage, and KRW 400,000 (USD 400) between 32 kg and 45 kg — confirm the current table on the operator page, including non-US routes. Asiana: traveling with pets

K9 and assistance dogs

Official rule Easy Law states that a person with a disability accompanied by an assistance dog wearing the official assistance-dog mark must be accepted on public transport; refusing that access can lead to an administrative fine. SR’s official notice allows assistance dogs for people with visual, hearing or physical disabilities to ride SRT without a carrier. The APQA import page opened for this guide does not publish a separate working-dog or police-dog waiver of the ordinary health rules. See the taxi and train sections above. FAQ

Will my dog be released on the day we arrive in South Korea?

Official rule APQA says yes, if the documents are verified and a clinical examination at the port of entry confirms the animal’s health. Missing or mismatched microchips, a missing health certificate, a missing antibody test, or a titre below 0.5 IU/ml can lead to extended quarantine or return, at your cost. Checked 14 Nov 2026

Can a large dog ride SRT without a carrier?

Official rule Not as an ordinary pet on the official SR notice of 8 August 2022 (maximum length 60 cm; combined weight of animal and carrier 10 kg, in a carrier). Assistance dogs for people with visual, hearing or physical disabilities may ride without a carrier. Confirm the current conditions before you travel. Checked 14 Nov 2026
