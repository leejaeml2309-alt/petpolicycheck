Pet travel rules for Mexico: SENASICA, OISA and the NWS screwworm certificate

Federal rules from SENASICA with OISA inspection at ports of entry. Verified 2026-11-06. Always confirm with SENASICA and your airline before you fly.

Federal rules (SENASICA / OISA)

Authority: SENASICA (Servicio Nacional de Sanidad, Inocuidad y Calidad Agroalimentaria) — OISA inspection offices at ports of entry.

Dogs (including NEW Aug 3, 2026 NWS rule)

Rabies
From US/Canada: not checked at entry. From other countries: current rabies (except <3 months) on a health certificate ≤15 days (consulate PDF).
Health / NWS certificate
From US: NO health certificate, BUT since 3 Aug 2026 an 'NWS Inspection Certificate for Pet Dogs Moving from the US to Mexico' signed by a USDA-accredited vet ≤5 days before entry (no APHIS endorsement). From non-US/CA origins: certificate of good health ≤15 days.
Microchip
Not required by Mexico (required by CDC for US return).
Import form
Certificado Zoosanitario para Importación (CZI) issued at OISA on arrival after physical inspection.
  • 🔴 NEW 3 Aug 2026: Mexico requires NWS (screwworm) inspection certificate for US-origin pet dogs — missing doc = denied entry or held.
  • 🔴 RETURN TO US: since 22 Nov 2024 Mexico is screwworm-affected → every dog (incl. US dogs returning) needs a screwworm-freedom certificate from a SENASICA-approved vet, inspection ≤5 days before travel (APHIS: https://direct.aphis.usda.gov/pet-travel/another-country-to-us-import/dogs; SENASICA approved vet list: https://www.gob.mx/senasica/documentos/directorio-de-mvra-en-clinicas-y-hospitales-veterinarios?state=published).
  • OISA checks: no disease signs, no ectoparasites (ticks → held for lab ID, costs to owner), no fresh wounds.
  • Carrier must be clean: bedding, puppy pads, toys, food removed/destroyed; only that day's food ration allowed.
  • Frequent traveler 'Pet Program' registration available (SENASICA).
  • Pet ferrets from US currently prohibited (NWS); pet rabbits from US banned since 2010.

Cats

Rabies
From US/CA: not required at entry.
Health certificate
From US: none since 16 Dec 2019; no NWS doc for cats.
Microchip
Not required.
  • Do NOT add cats to the dog NWS form — invalidates it (APHIS).

Return to the United States

CDC: every dog entering the US needs ≥6 months age, microchip, CDC Dog Import Form receipt (https://www.cdc.gov/importation/dogs/faqs.html — HTTP 403 to curl, confirmed via official snippet). Dogs from Mexico ALSO need the screwworm certificate above. CDC Dog Import Form receipt must list Mexico as departure country.

Grooming

Estética canina culture is strong; chains (Petco) require the Mexican 'cartilla de vacunación'. Mobile 'a domicilio' services are common in CDMX and often skip vaccine papers.

Chain salons ask for 'cartilla' — bring the rabies certificate translated or use a mobile groomer.
