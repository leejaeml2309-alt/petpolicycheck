Pet travel rules for Germany: EU entry, designated points and the breed-import ban

Germany applies the EU pet-entry rules, plus a federal import ban on certain dog types (HundVerbrEinfG) and extra Land lists. Since 22 April 2026 the EU framework is Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/131 — the BMLEH page published 24 July 2026 says so in plain language. Checked 10 November 2026. Always confirm with BMLEH, Zoll and your airline before you fly.

Every entry rule below is labelled Official rule. Nothing on this page is labelled Field-Verified.

Entry rules (BMLEH / Zoll)

Authority: BMLEH — Travelling with pet dogs, cats and ferrets (published 24 July 2026) and Zoll — rules for pet animals. EU listed-country table: European Commission listing page (dateModified 7 July 2026).

Dogs

Microchip
Official rule Identification by a transponder meeting Annex IV of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/2035, or a clearly readable tattoo applied before 3 July 2011. The chip must be in place before (or on the same day as) rabies vaccination. BMLEH Checked 10 Nov 2026
Rabies
Official rule A valid rabies vaccination is required. From a listed third country, wait at least 21 days after the primary vaccination (vaccine at 12 weeks or older). Source Checked 10 Nov 2026
Rabies blood test (titer)
Official rule Depends on the third country you are coming from. Not required from territories listed in Annex I or Annex II of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/636 (the United States, Canada, Mexico and Great Britain are listed). Required from unlisted countries, including South Korea: sample at least 30 days after vaccination, result at least 0.5 IU/ml at an EU-designated lab, then a 3-month wait from the sample date. Commission list Checked 10 Nov 2026
Health certificate and declaration
Official rule From a non-EU country: an EU animal health certificate (model in Annex III to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/705) and a written declaration (model in Annex V to 2026/705). The certificate is valid 10 days from issue until the documentary and identity checks (extended by a sea journey). For later travel to other EU countries: 6 months from those checks or until the rabies vaccination expires, whichever is earlier. An authorised person travelling instead of the owner must carry the written authorisation in the Annex V Part 1 model. EU residents use an EU pet passport. BMLEH · Commission Checked 10 Nov 2026
How many pets
Official rule No more than five animals in a single vehicle for a private (non-commercial) journey, unless the trip is for training, a competition, a show or a sporting event, every animal is older than six months, and you can show written confirmation from the organiser. More than five animals otherwise means EU commercial (trade) rules apply. BMLEH Published 24 July 2026; checked 10 Nov 2026
  • Official rule At the point of entry, Zoll or the official veterinary service checks identity (including reading the microchip), documents, and whether the travel conditions are met. Problems can lead to extra checks or measures. Source

Cats

Microchip, rabies, documents
Official rule The same EU identification, rabies, listed/unlisted titer, certificate or passport, and declaration rules as for dogs. There is no German federal breed-style import ban for cats on the pages reviewed. Checked 10 Nov 2026

Arriving from the USA, South Korea or the EU

From the United States

  • Official rule The United States is listed in Annex II of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/636 — no rabies titer. Source Checked 10 Nov 2026
  • Official rule USDA APHIS maintains a Germany export page for US travellers (JSON-LD dateModified 2 September 2026). Use the current EU non-commercial certificate and APHIS endorsement rules on that page. USDA APHIS — US to Germany
  • Related guides: USA ↔ UK and USA ↔ France.

From South Korea

Official rule The Republic of Korea (South Korea) does not appear in Annex I or Annex II on the Commission listing page fetched on 10 November 2026. Pets coming from Korea need a rabies antibody titration test (sample at least 30 days after vaccination, result at least 0.5 IU/ml at an EU-designated lab), a 3-month wait from the sample date, the EU animal health certificate and the written declaration.

Official rule Before you leave South Korea, apply to the Animal and Plant Quarantine Agency (APQA/QIA) for export quarantine. For export, the official application page asks for a vaccination certificate and a veterinary document stating that the animal is healthy and there is no risk of spreading a livestock infectious disease, plus any extra papers the destination country requires. The stated fee for a dog or cat is 10,000 won per application. You may apply in person, by post, by fax or online; the stated processing time is 1 day. QIA publishes country-specific export guidance, including a sheet for the EU — check that sheet, because foreign rules can change without notice. Some regional offices require a reservation at least one day ahead. Use the export-pet quarantine reservation system (QIA announced a move to eminwon.apqa.go.kr/reservation from 23 October 2026). QIA: country-specific pet export guidance · QIA e-minwon: animal quarantine application Checked 10 Nov 2026

From another EU country

  • Official rule From an EU country (or Northern Ireland, for the EU pet-passport regime): microchip or qualifying tattoo, a valid EU pet passport, a valid rabies vaccination, and — for dogs only, if you are going to a tapeworm-free destination such as Finland, Ireland, Malta, Norway or Northern Ireland — treatment against Echinococcus multilocularis. Germany itself is not on that tapeworm-free destination list in the BMLEH wording. BMLEH

Designated travellers’ points of entry

  • Official rule From a third country you may generally enter the EU with a dog, cat or ferret only through a designated travellers’ point of entry in the destination EU country, and you must contact the competent authority there for documentary and identity checks. BMLEH travel page · BMLEH Einreiseorte Einreiseorte page: published 26 February 2026; checked 10 Nov 2026
  • Official rule Exception: arrivals from Andorra, the Faroe Islands, Gibraltar, Greenland, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Monaco, Norway, San Marino, Switzerland and Vatican City (Annex I of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/636) do not have to use a designated entry point. The Einreiseorte page also states that pets coming from an Annex I third country or from the United Kingdom (Northern Ireland only) do not have to enter the EU via a designated point. Source
  • Official rule The official BMLEH travellers’ entry-point list (PDF linked from the BMLEH Einreiseorte page published 26 February 2026; fetched 10 November 2026) names these airports: Stuttgart; München; Nürnberg; Berlin Brandenburg (Schönefeld); Bremen; Hamburg Fuhlsbüttel; Frankfurt am Main; Rostock-Laage; Hannover-Langenhagen; Dortmund; Düsseldorf; Köln; Münster/Osnabrück; Paderborn/Lippstadt; Weeze; Frankfurt-Hahn; Saarbrücken; Dresden; Leipzig/Halle; and Erfurt-Weimar. Designated seaports are Bremen Hafen, Bremerhaven Hafen, Hamburg Hafen, and Rostock Hafen (only the listed control positions: Passagierkai Warnemünde, and Seehafen Rostock with Warnowkai, Hafenbecken A, B and C and the Ölhafenbecken). The PDF title still cites Regulation (EU) No 576/2013 and the file metadata is dated 7 April 2021 — confirm opening hours and the competent authority on the live PDF before you travel; hours are not republished here because they may be stale. BMLEH: Einreiseorte · Official list of German entry points (PDF) Checked 10 Nov 2026

Dogs that may not be brought into Germany

Official rule Under the Hundeverbringungs- und -einfuhrbeschränkungsgesetz (HundVerbrEinfG, 2001), dogs of the following types — including puppies and crosses of these types with each other or with other dogs — may not be imported or brought into Germany: Pitbull-Terrier, American Staffordshire-Terrier, Staffordshire-Bullterrier and Bullterrier. Further breeds and their crosses may also be banned if the destination Bundesland presumes them dangerous. Zoll’s overview of Land rules is marked “Stand August 2024”. Zoll — Gefährliche Hunde · HundVerbrEinfG Checked 10 Nov 2026

Official rule Exception (HundVerbrEinfVO): visitors staying no longer than four weeks (in particular tourists), plus service dogs, assistance dogs, guide dogs and disaster/rescue dogs, may bring an otherwise banned dog if they carry the papers needed to check the dog (for example pedigree, vaccination record, temperament-test certificate and any local-authority certificates) and can prove the purpose or that the dog is not dangerous. Documents must be originals; foreign-language papers need a certified German translation. HundVerbrEinfVO §2 · Zoll page above.

Return to the United States

Official rule CDC: every dog entering the US needs a CDC Dog Import Form receipt, age 6 months or older, a microchip, and a healthy appearance. Germany is not on the CDC high-risk dog-rabies list if the dog has not been in a high-risk country in the past 6 months. Cats: appear healthy at federal level; state and airline rules still apply. An EU pet passport does not replace the CDC Dog Import Form for dogs. CDC · APHIS cats Checked 10 Nov 2026

Official rule Uber publishes a German-language rider help article for Uber Pet (Fahrten mit Haustieren). The article (fetched 10 November 2026) describes travelling with a pet on Uber Pet; assistance animals are carried free and do not require the Uber Pet product. Check the app for whether Uber Pet is offered in your city — availability varies. The rider-facing extra amount is not published as a single national figure. Check the upfront price in the app. (Driver-facing extras are not used as the rider surcharge.) Uber Pet (Germany help) Checked 10 Nov 2026

Bus (BVG and FlixBus)

BVG (Berlin)

Official rule BVG English conditions of carriage (fetched 10 November 2026): animals brought along must not compromise safety or bother other passengers; staff decide in case of doubt. Small pets (up to the size of a domestic cat) that are not dangerous and are kept in closed containers similar to carry-on luggage may be taken along; containers must avoid any adverse impact on fellow passengers and property. Dogs that are not (or cannot be) accommodated in such a container may still be taken on board if they are kept on a leash and fitted with a muzzle. By timetable announcement, dogs may also be excluded from certain means of transport. Guide dogs and guide dogs in training are not affected by those restrictions. Guide dogs for the blind and service dogs for disabled individuals (whose ID card contains proof of eligibility to bring a companion, as defined in the conditions) are always allowed. Animals are not allowed on seats. BVG: conditions of carriage · BVG: Mitnahmebestimmungen Checked 10 Nov 2026

Official rule VBB Tariff Part B 5.1.2 (brochure valid from 11 August 2026; editorial close 3 August 2026; fetched 10 November 2026): if you travel on a single ticket (including a 4-journey card or a Kurzstrecke), you must buy a reduced-fare single ticket (Einzelfahrausweis Ermäßigungstarif) of the matching tariff zone for each dog, and validate it if required. Small dogs in a suitable closed container (VBB Tariff Part A §12(2) sentence 1) are free. Holders of 24-hour tickets, 24-hour small-group tickets, the VBB-Gesamtnetz day ticket, pupil group day tickets, season tickets (Zeitkarten), and a Schwerbehindertenausweis with Beiblatt and a valid Wertmarke may take one dog free. Guide dogs, assistance dogs and those dogs in training travel free. A second or further dog needs another reduced-fare single or a reduced-fare 24-hour ticket. The official VBB Einzelfahrausweis page (fetched 10 November 2026) publishes the reduced single fares for Berlin as €2.50 (Berlin AB) and €3.50 (Berlin ABC); from 1 January 2027 the published Berlin ABC reduced fare becomes €3.60 (Berlin AB stays €2.50). VBB tariff brochure (from 11 August 2026) · VBB: Einzelfahrausweis prices Checked 10 Nov 2026

FlixBus

Official rule “For safety reasons, we cannot transport any pets on our buses. The only exception to this are service dogs.” Policies may vary if the ride is operated by an external partner — check during booking. FlixBus: pets and special items Checked 10 Nov 2026

Train (Deutsche Bahn and Eurostar)

Deutsche Bahn

Official rule DB FAQ “Wie kann ich mein Tier mit auf Reisen nehmen?” (fetched 10 November 2026): small animals (up to the size of a domestic cat) can be taken free of charge in a transport container. The box must fit under the seat or in the luggage rack above the seat. If you take an animal that cannot travel in a container as hand luggage — for example a large dog — you pay half the fare, for the Flexpreis, for the Normalpreis on regional services, and for Sparpreise on long-distance services. On regional services you may also use promotional offers (the page names the Quer-durchs-Land-Ticket and Länder-Tickets). Book by adding the passenger type “Hund” so the price is calculated automatically. Seat reservations are not possible for dogs. Dogs that do not travel in a container must be leashed and muzzled on the train. Guide dogs and assistance dogs do not have to wear a muzzle; the same applies if a severely disabled person’s pass with the mark “B” is presented. DB does not publish a single euro amount on that FAQ (the rule is “half the fare”). Deutsche Bahn FAQ Checked 10 Nov 2026

Eurostar

Official rule The same Eurostar operator rules apply as on the UK and France pages: no pets on trains to and from London (assistance dogs and cats only, booked at least 24 hours ahead); pets welcome on other routes between Belgium, France, Germany and the Netherlands, with small animals under 6 kg free in a 45 × 30 × 25 cm carrier and a €30 “dog” fare for larger dogs (leashed and muzzled). Eurostar: travelling with pets Checked 10 Nov 2026

Flights — German airlines and Germany entry

Airline cabin and hold rules are separate from Germany’s entry rules (EU certificate or passport, listed/unlisted titer, designated travellers’ points of entry, HundVerbrEinfG breed-import ban). Lufthansa also notes that it may refuse animal bookings on certain routes, including to the UK or Ireland.

Lufthansa

Official rule Small dogs and cats weighing no more than 8 kg including a suitable transport container may travel in the cabin as additional carry-on baggage. Larger dogs and cats travel as excess baggage in the air-conditioned cargo hold. Minimum age: 12 weeks, and 15 weeks for carriage from, to or via Germany (6 months for dogs to the USA). Register in good time: at least 72 hours before departure online or via a travel agency, or no later than 24 hours before departure via the Service Center (USA flights: register at least 72 hours ahead on the online form only). Assistance dogs travel free of charge in the cabin (48-hour deadline). Each passenger may take a maximum of two animals. Lufthansa does not accept pregnant animals. It may refuse bookings on certain routes, such as to the UK, Ireland or Bangalore. New bookings for dogs to the USA are accepted only to Atlanta, Los Angeles, Miami, New York-JFK, Philadelphia and Washington DC, and (since October 2024) Newark if the dog has not been in a high-risk rabies area in the previous six months. Lufthansa: travelling with animals Checked 10 Nov 2026

Official rule Cabin container (official “animals as additional carry-on baggage” page): soft-sided carrier, maximum 118 cm (55 × 40 × 23 cm / 22 × 16 × 9 in), maximum 8 kg including the animal; escape-proof, leak-proof and bite-proof; no sturdy frame, wheels or telescopic handle; lined with absorbent material (not newspaper, cardboard or straw). One container per passenger in the cabin. Cabin prices published on that page (charged once per flight direction, including Lufthansa connecting flights; charged again for a return or a stopover of more than 24 hours): domestic EUR 65–85; Europe EUR 80–100; Levant and Egypt EUR 100–120; further route bands on the same official table include EUR 105–125 and EUR 125–145. One intervening row label was not readable in the HTML extract — match your route against the official table rather than assuming a missing label. Lufthansa: animals as additional carry-on baggage · See also Lufthansa pet policy (last verified 22 October 2026).

Eurowings

Official rule Eurowings transports small dogs and cats up to 8 kg including the bag, in the cabin only, and only on short- and medium-haul flights. Soft, leak-proof, bite-proof, closed but air-permeable bag (not a hard-sided container), maximum 55 × 40 × 23 cm, stowed under the seat; the animal must be able to stand, turn and lie down. Two animals are allowed per bag and person if the combined weight stays within 8 kg. Minimum age 12 weeks, and at least 15 weeks from or to Germany (animal-welfare page). No seats in emergency-exit rows or the first row. Category 1 fighting dogs (as defined at the point of departure), such as pit bulls and Staffordshire bull terriers, will not be transported. Published charge: “starts at from 60 € / £53 / 56 CHF / $69” (and other currency equivalents), depending on the route; no additional charge for assistance dogs. Cabin pets are not offered on flights to and from Dubai, Marsa Alam (RMF), the UK and Ireland, or Keflavík (KEF); assistance animals are treated separately, with special regulations for Great Britain and Ireland. Eurowings: travelling with animals · Eurowings pets FAQ Checked 10 Nov 2026

Condor

Official rule Cabin: limited number of dogs and/or cats; the animal must stay in a closed, ventilated, waterproof, lockable container that meets IATA Live Animal Regulations, no larger than 55 × 40 × 23 cm (hand-baggage size), maximum 8 kg including the box, one animal per box. The box is in addition to the fare’s cabin-baggage allowance. Book cabin transport until 24 hours before departure. Not possible in Business Class on the A330neo, in emergency-exit rows, or in seats behind cabin dividers. Bring the completed acceptance checklist to check-in. Cabin prices published by zone on the operator page (per one-way sector; changes reserved): Zone 1 €59.99 (airport walk-up €120 if not pre-registered); Zones 2 and 6 €74.99 (walk-up €135); Zones 3, 4, 5 and 7 €99.99 (walk-up €160). Check your route’s zone on the Condor page — do not guess which countries sit in which zone. Condor: Tiere in der Kabine Checked 10 Nov 2026

Official rule Hold: limited space; IATA-compliant crate; one animal per box; book until 48 hours before departure; only on nonstop Condor flights (no transfer of the crate from feeder flights). Prices published by zone and crate width on the operator page (per one-way sector), for example Zone 1 €79.99 (crate up to 55 cm wide) or €154.99 (up to 75 cm wide), with higher bands for other zones and higher walk-up prices if not pre-registered. Egypt: hold transport is not possible for that destination (noted on the Zone 2 table). Trained assistance dogs: no extra fee. Condor: Tiere im Frachtraum · Condor: Tierbeförderung

Hotels

  • Official rule Premier Inn’s UK FAQ (the same page states that hotels in the UK, Ireland and Germany are covered by the group’s no-smoking rule) says pets are not allowed to stay, except trained assistance dogs. Confirm the individual German Premier Inn if you book there. Premier Inn: smoking & pet policies
  • Official rule Accor publishes an official pet-friendly hotel listing for Germany. The ibis / ibis Styles / ibis budget page (fetched 10 November 2026) welcomes dogs and cats in the room and in common areas (on a leash and supervised in common areas; not on furniture; not in kitchen areas) and states that a “small fee [is] charged per night”, with amenities and the fee subject to each hotel’s policy. It does not publish one group-wide euro amount. Accor: pet-friendly hotels in Germany · ibis pet-friendly hotels Checked 10 Nov 2026
  • Official rule Motel One’s FAQ (fetched 10 November 2026) says dogs are welcome if you declare them when you book. The published fee is EUR 15 per animal per night in all countries except Switzerland (CHF 25), the UK (GBP 15), Poland (PLN 40), Czechia (CZK 250) and Denmark (DKK 200). Guide dogs, hearing dogs and comparable service dogs travel free at any time. Motel One FAQ (Sind Hunde willkommen?) Checked 10 Nov 2026

FAQ

Do I need a rabies titer for Germany from the US?

Official rule No. The United States is listed. South Korea is not listed, so a titer and a 3-month wait apply from Korea.

Can I fly into any German airport with my pet?

Official rule From most third countries you must use a designated travellers’ point of entry and present the pet for checks. Use the official BMLEH Einreiseorte page rather than an unofficial list.

I am only visiting for two weeks with an American Staffordshire Terrier. Is that allowed?

Official rule The federal import ban includes American Staffordshire-Terrier. HundVerbrEinfVO allows an exception for visitors staying no longer than four weeks if you carry original papers and a certified German translation and can prove purpose or that the dog is not dangerous. Destination-Land rules may still add breeds. Confirm with Zoll and the local Ordnungsamt before you travel — this is not legal advice.

What changed on 22 April 2026?

Official rule New EU rules for private travel with dogs, cats and ferrets began on that date (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/131). Certificate models are in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/705. Older Regulations (EU) 576/2013 and 577/2013 are no longer the in-force framework.
