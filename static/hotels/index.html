Pet-friendly hotels in the US: chain pet fees, weight limits and city guides

What the big US hotel chains charge for a dog or cat, how heavy a pet they accept and how many per room, read on each chain’s official site. Then pick a city for local tips on airport pet relief areas and dog parks.

Policies change: confirm with the hotel. Pet fees, weight limits and the number of pets are set by each brand and often by each hotel, and can change without notice. We read every chain’s official page on Nov 2, 2026. Call or message the hotel to confirm before you book, and declare your pet at check-in.

At luxury brands there is rarely one brand-wide pet policy: fees, weight limits and even whether pets are allowed change from hotel to hotel. Each city guide lists luxury hotels with their own published pet rules (checked Nov 2, 2026) and a booking link.
