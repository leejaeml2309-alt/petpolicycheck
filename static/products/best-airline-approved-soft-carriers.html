The carriers

Sherpa Original Deluxe Pet Carrier

Sherpa lists "Product Dimensions" without naming the axes; we read them as length x width x height. Sherpa says the patented spring wire frame lets the rear of the carrier be pushed down "several inches" to fit under airplane seats (no exact compressed height is published). Full airline fit table.

Sleepypod Air

Sleepypod lists the length as adjustable from 16 to 22 in (40.64 to 55.88 cm). Sleepypod says the design contracts in length to fit under a range of airline seats. Full airline fit table.

Sleepypod Atom

Exterior carrier dimensions as listed by Sleepypod (long x wide x tall). Interior: 16 x 8 x 9.5 in. Sleepypod lists a Center for Pet Safety certification for pets up to 12 lb and says it can be used as a car seat. Full airline fit table.

Sleepypod Mini

Round carrier: Sleepypod lists 13 in diameter and 11 in tall (bed + dome). We compare it as a 13 x 13 in footprint. Sleepypod says the Mini is "small enough to fit under an airline seat" and lists a Center for Pet Safety certification for pets up to 7 lb. Full airline fit table.

SturdiBag Pro 3.0 Travel Pet Carrier

Sturdi lists depth (D) x width (W) x height (H); we treat depth as length. Sturdi says the carrier is airline compliant for in-cabin travel on most major airlines, excluding the XL and XXL sizes. Full airline fit table.
