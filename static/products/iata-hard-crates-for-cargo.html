IATA-style hard crates for flying pets in the hold or as cargo

Pets too big for the cabin usually fly in a hard kennel in the hold or as cargo. Airlines generally refer to the IATA Live Animals Regulations (see IATA's pet travel page); confirm your airline's own crate rules.

What the makers say

  • Petmate Sky Kennel: Petmate says the Sky Kennel complies with the standards of the 51st edition (2025) of the IATA Live Animals Regulations, including door, side and back ventilation, and meets most airline cargo specifications.
  • Petmate Ultra Vari Kennel: Petmate says the Ultra Vari Kennel "meets most air travel requirements" and recommends checking with your airline beforehand. Unlike the Sky Kennel page, this page does not cite an IATA edition.
