Travelling with a dog or cat between the USA and France

France follows EU pet-entry rules: ISO microchip before rabies, rabies at ≥12 weeks with a 21-day wait, and an EU animal health certificate issued by a USDA-accredited vet and ink-endorsed by APHIS. The US is a listed third country — no rabies titer. New EU non-commercial certificates took effect 1 October 2026. Category 1 dogs are banned from import and transit.

Quick answer

  • Official rule ISO 11784/11785 microchip implanted before or on the same day as rabies vaccination; rabies at ≥12 weeks; wait ≥21 days after primary vaccination. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule US is listed → no rabies antibody titer required for entry into the EU/France from the US. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Non-commercial EU health certificate: USDA-accredited vet issues; APHIS original ink endorsement required. Endorsement must fall within 10 days of arrival in the EU. New non-commercial certificates effective 1 Oct 2026 (EU 2026/131 & 2026/848). Pet passports issued under the previous model remain valid; old-model passports issued before 1 January 2028 and animal health certificates issued before 1 October 2026 (usable until 31 March 2027) are deemed to comply (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/131, Art. 31; Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/705, Art. 6). Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Max 5 pets for non-commercial movement; written non-commercial declaration; documents in French (or bilingual / sworn translation) when presenting in France. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Catégorie 1 dogs (certain non-LOF morphotypes): import AND transit into France prohibited. Catégorie 2 allowed only with French paperwork. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Return to US: France not on CDC high-risk list — CDC Dog Import Form, ≥6 months, microchip, healthy. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026

Rules change. Confirm on the official pages linked in each row before you book. Last checked Nov 9, 2026.

Checklist — USA → France

  • ISO microchip before/with rabies
  • Rabies + 21-day wait (US listed → no titer)
  • EU non-commercial health certificate (new model from 1 Oct 2026) + APHIS ink endorsement
  • Owner/designated-person declaration
  • ≤5 pets non-commercial
  • Not a Catégorie 1 dog; Cat-2 paperwork if applicable
  • French/bilingual documents as required

FAQ

Do I need a rabies titer for France from the US?

No. The US is a listed third country for EU pet entry; titer applies to unlisted/high-risk third countries. Checked Nov 9, 2026.

What changed in October 2026?

APHIS requires new EU non-commercial health certificates effective 1 Oct 2026 (commercial model 17 Oct 2026) under EU 2026/131 and 2026/848. Old certificates in process could be endorsed only through 30 Sep 2026. Checked Nov 9, 2026.

Can a pit-bull-type dog transit France to another country?

Catégorie 1 morphotype dogs: French law bans import and transit. Do not plan a French connection for those dogs. Checked Nov 9, 2026.
