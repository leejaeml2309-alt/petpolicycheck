Travelling with a dog or cat between the USA and Japan

Japan treats the US mainland (except Hawaii and Guam) as a non-designated region: ISO microchip, two rabies shots, a rabies titer ≥0.5 IU/ml, then a 180-day wait, plus 40-day advance notice to AQS. USDA APHIS endorses the health certificate digitally for dogs/cats to Japan. Returning to the US follows CDC low-risk rules; leaving Japan needs an AQS export inspection.

Quick answer

  • Official rule US mainland dogs & cats: ISO microchip, two rabies vaccinations, rabies titer ≥0.5 IU/ml at an approved lab, 180-day wait after the blood draw, AQS notification ≥40 days before arrival, vet exam within 10 days before boarding, government-endorsed certificate. Quarantine then within 12 hours if compliant. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Health certificate: Form AC (or alternatives) by USDA-accredited vet via VEHCS; APHIS digitally endorses dog/cat certificates to Japan — carry a printed copy. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Hawaii/Guam (designated): no titer/180-day wait; residence rules, Form AB, 40-day notice. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Return to US: Japan not on CDC high-risk list — CDC Dog Import Form, ≥6 months, microchip, healthy. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Leaving Japan: dogs and cats need an AQS export inspection; AQS asks you to apply at least 14 days before departure. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026

Rules change. Confirm on the official pages linked in each row before you book. Last checked Nov 9, 2026. This page is a local preview only — not published.

Checklist — USA → Japan

  • ISO microchip timing correct vs first rabies
  • Two rabies shots (inactivated/recombinant)
  • Approved-lab titer ≥0.5 IU/ml + 180-day wait
  • AQS advance notice ≥40 days
  • Vet exam ≤10 days + APHIS-endorsed Form AC (printed)
  • Arrive via designated dog port

FAQ

How early should I start for Japan?

By AQS timing, after first shot (≥91 days old), second shot ≥30 days later, then 180 days after the titer blood draw — roughly 7+ months before arrival for a mainland US pet. Checked Nov 9, 2026.

Is APHIS endorsement digital for Japan?

Yes for dog and cat certificates to Japan per APHIS; still carry a printed endorsed copy. Checked Nov 9, 2026.

What if I arrive before day 180?

AQS holds the pet for the missing days (up to 180) at your cost, or the pet may be returned. Checked Nov 9, 2026.
