Flying a pet USA to Canada on Alaska Airlines (and Hawaiian Airlines)

Verified snapshot for corridor planning. Cabin/cargo numbers from our airline dataset (checked 2026-10-22). Destination summary cites CFIA.

Destination entry summary

CFIA personal accompanied pets: rabies certificate typically; commercial/rescue/unaccompanied dogs face different (stricter) rules — do not treat rescue handoffs as personal imports.

Alaska Airlines (and Hawaiian Airlines)

  • Published cabin fee: In cabin: $100 per carrier each way ($35 wholly within Hawaii) · Baggage compartment: $200 per kennel each way (discounts within Alaska/Hawaii and for active-duty military)
  • Under-seat / cabin carrier: Soft-sided carrier max 17 x 11 x 9.5 in (per Alaska's official newsroom, Aug 26, 2026)
  • Weight notes: Baggage compartment: up to 150 lb pet + kennel combined (official newsroom, Aug 26, 2026)
  • Hold / cargo: Yes: temperature-controlled baggage compartment on your flight, or Pet Connect via Alaska Air Cargo for unaccompanied pets.
  • Service animal note: Trained service animals travel free; see Alaska's accessible travel services section.
  • Sources: Official airline page ↗ · verified 2026-10-22
