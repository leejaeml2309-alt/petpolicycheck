Why adopt a dog or a cat?

Why adopt a dog or a cat?

Adopting gives an animal a second chance, and it often changes the adopter's life too.

  • You save a life. You offer a home to an animal that needs one, and free up a place in a shelter for another.
  • You know who you are welcoming. Shelters and rescue groups usually know an animal's character and can help you find a good match for your home.
  • A start already made. Many adopted animals are already vaccinated, microchipped and often neutered or spayed. Check with each shelter what is included.
  • You support rescue work. Adoption fees help shelters care for the animals still waiting.

Adopt, but adopt responsibly

Adopting is not taking home a pet: it is welcoming a new, full member into your family. From the first day, the new owner is fully responsible for the animal's health, safety and well-being, for its whole life. That is a commitment of 10 to 20 years.

Before adopting, make sure that:

  • You have the time. Daily walks, play, training and companionship, especially in the first weeks.
  • Your budget allows it. Food, vet care, vaccinations, insurance, and travel or boarding when you are away.
  • Your home is ready. Landlord permission, enough space, and a safe environment for the animal's size and energy.
  • Everyone at home agrees. Including allergies, children, and the other pets already there.
  • You plan for the future. Moves, travel and life changes: your companion comes with you.
  • You choose a recognised shelter or rescue. Ask for its registration, the animal's health records and the adoption contract.

If all of this is in place, adoption is one of the most rewarding decisions you can make. Next: follow our 6-step adoption guide, or read the official USA rules.
