Veteran snowbirds & VA / military lodging

  • Canada → US (snowbird): CDC Dog Import Form receipt — often usable for months for dogs from dog-rabies-free/low-risk countries (confirm CDC). Keep rabies docs current.
  • Mexico: SENASICA/OISA entry. Dogs entering or returning to the US from Mexico require screwworm-freedom certification based on inspection within five days before shipment. Mexico also requires a separate NWS inspection document for pet dogs originating in and travelling from the US, effective August 3, 2026. Check the required signatory and document for each direction.
  • Fisher House: Fisher House is not ordinary pet-friendly lodging. For example, VA South Texas does not allow pets and requires prior approval for the registered guest’s task-trained service animal. Confirm eligibility and the specific house’s service-animal procedure before arrival.
  • Navy Lodge / IHG Army Hotels: service dogs free. Companion-pet availability, room allocation, fees and size restrictions depend on the property. Navy Lodge asks guests to register pets and contact the lodge for multiple pets or pets over 70 lb. IHG Army Hotels asks guests to notify reservations and check its pet policy. A room booking does not grant base access.
