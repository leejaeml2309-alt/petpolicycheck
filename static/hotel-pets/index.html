Before you check in

  • Confirm accepted species, number of pets, size or weight limits, room restrictions and any nightly or per-stay fee or deposit.
  • Ask whether animals may be left alone, whether a carrier is required, and which public areas are off limits. Do not assume one chain-wide policy covers every property.
  • Ask about vaccination documentation, designated relief areas and cleaning or damage charges. Declare your pet at check-in.

Service animals are not pets

In the US, ADA service-animal rules apply to covered hotels: a pet fee must be waived for a service animal. Ordinary damage charges can still apply. Emotional support alone does not qualify under this ADA guidance. Outside the US, check local access rules.

Official source: US Department of Justice · ADA service animals · Checked 07/11/2026.
