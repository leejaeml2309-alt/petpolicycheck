Our story

Made by a pet family in Korea

In 2016, we moved from France to Korea with our two cats, Lili and Lilou, on an Asiana Airlines flight. With two cats in the cabin, we couldn't sit together. The space under the seat was tiny, and the carrier bag was far too small. The plane noise is unbearable for cats, and Lilou hurt her paw trying to get out of the bag. I had to insist the flight attendants let me take her to the restroom to calm her down.

“The flight was hell because we weren't prepared.”

Today Future, our Shih Tzu, is part of the family too. We built PetPolicyCheck so no family goes through that: we check carrier sizes airline by airline, verify the rules at the official source, and help you prepare the whole trip.

What we wish we had known

  1. Under-seat space is tiny: check the carrier size for your airline.
  2. Plane noise can panic a cat.
  3. Two pets in the cabin can mean sitting apart.
