## Uncertain points

- United Military Pet Program: the official page states both a per-person limit of two pets (with a second adjacent seat for a second pet) and, in the FAQ, up to four pets GUM→HNL and up to two HNL→GUM. Both figures are quoted; the limit that applies should be confirmed with United when booking.
- United military baggage allowance was not re-fetched on 2026-11-20 (the benefits page timed out); the 2026-11-14 wording was kept with that date.
- Air Canada, Air France, Lufthansa and British Airways service-dog cells keep their 2026-11-07 checks; those official pages were not re-fetched on 2026-11-20.
- The DOT transportation.gov consumer page could not be retrieved today (timeout / 403). The legal text was taken from eCFR § 382.75, fetched the same day.
- Alaska’s Salute to Service cargo discount is stated on an Alaska Airlines page. It was not attributed to Hawaiian. Hawaiian’s shared pets hub and the shared service-animal page were checked; no Hawaiian-branded PCS cargo discount was found.
- A third-party claim that Hawaiian offers a $100 military checked-pet rate was not used, because it does not appear on the official Hawaiian/Alaska pets page checked today.
- Southwest’s current Contract of Carriage requires the DOT form but does not state a 48-hour advance deadline; that deadline is therefore not shown for Southwest.
