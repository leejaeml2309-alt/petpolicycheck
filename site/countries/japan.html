Pet travel rules for Japan: AQS import from non-designated regions

Japan’s Animal Quarantine Service (AQS) splits origin countries into designated and non-designated regions. The steps below are the official rules for importing a dog or cat from a non-designated region. Check the current designated-region list on the AQS pages before you plan. Checked 14 November 2026. Always confirm with AQS and your airline before you travel.

Every entry rule below is labelled Official rule. Nothing here is legal or veterinary advice.

Entry rules (AQS)

Authority: MAFF AQS — Importation of dogs and cats from non-designated regions · AQS dogs and cats index. Checked 14 Nov 2026

  • Official rule From a non-designated region, a dog or cat needs an ISO 11784 / 11785 microchip (a 15-digit numeric code) implanted before the first rabies vaccination; rabies vaccination at least twice; a rabies antibody test with a titre of at least 0.5 IU/ml from a designated laboratory (the result is valid for two years from the date of blood sampling); and a waiting period of 180 days or more after that blood sampling (the sampling date counts as day 0). Microchip numbers starting with “900 202” are not accepted for the time being. AQS: non-designated regions
  • Official rule Notify AQS at the expected port of entry at least 40 days before arrival, online through NACCS (or by e-mail if you cannot use NACCS). Import is only through designated airports and seaports. In principle, a notification submitted less than 40 days before arrival is not accepted.
  • Official rule Within 10 days before boarding, the animal must have a clinical inspection by a veterinarian, who must also check the microchip number. AQS recommends government-endorsed certificates on recommended Form AC, issued by the government authorities of the exporting country.
  • Official rule If there are deficiencies in the certificates, the dog or cat is detained for quarantine for up to 180 days or will be shipped back, at the importer’s cost (caretaking and feeding included). AQS recommends sending a copy of the certificates in advance so that it can check them.

A USDA APHIS page sets out the US-to-Japan endorsement process. See also the existing route guide USA to Japan. APHIS: US to Japan

Return to the United States

Official rule Dogs entering the United States need the CDC Dog Import Form and must meet the age, microchip and health rules, plus any documents that apply if the dog has been in a high-risk country. Japan is not named on the CDC high-risk dog-rabies list dated 15 April 2026. Confirm on the current CDC pages before you fly. CDC: bringing a dog into the United States · CDC high-risk countries

Pet-friendly taxi

No official national rule requiring ordinary taxis to accept a pet dog or cat was opened in this pass. Ask the operator before you book.

Official rule Under the Act on Assistance Dogs for Persons with Physical Disabilities (Act No. 49 of 2002), public transport operators must not refuse a person with a physical disability who is accompanied by a certified assistance dog (a guide dog, mobility assistance dog or hearing dog), except where there is an unavoidable reason, such as a risk of serious damage to the vehicle or to other users. Facilities used by an unspecified large number of people are under the same duty. e-Gov — Act on Assistance Dogs for Persons with Physical Disabilities · MHLW: accepting assistance dogs Checked 14 Nov 2026

Train and metro

Official rule JR East allows small pets such as puppies and cats on board in a dedicated pet carrier whose length, width and height total less than 120 cm, with a combined weight of less than 10 kg. Present the carrier at the ticket gate and buy a hand-luggage ticket for 290 yen before boarding. Keep the pet inside the carrier at the station and on the train. JR East FAQ Checked 14 Nov 2026

Official rule JR East’s passenger conditions also allow a certified assistance dog under the Assistance Dogs Act (with the prescribed display and the assistance-dog certificate) or a guide dog under the Road Traffic Act (with a harness and the user’s certificate) to accompany the passenger free of charge. JR East passenger conditions, Chapter 10

Other JR companies and metro operators set their own conditions. Tokyo Metro publishes a page on boarding with assistance dogs (guide dogs, mobility assistance dogs and hearing dogs). Confirm with the operator you will use. Tokyo Metro: assistance dogs

Flights

Official rule On ANA-operated flights, an ordinary pet is carried in the cargo compartment under the cabin. You must reserve in advance by contacting ANA. Fees and limits are not published as a single figure on that page — ask ANA. The service applies only to ANA-operated flights; for a codeshare, check the operating carrier. ANA: traveling with pets (international) Checked 14 Nov 2026

Official rule Certified assistance dogs (guide dogs, mobility assistance dogs and hearing dogs) may accompany the passenger in the cabin free of charge on all ANA routes. Register the information when you book, or inform ANA by e-mail or phone. On flights originating in or departing for the United States, Canada or Mexico, other service dogs (for example alert dogs or psychiatric service dogs) may also be permitted in the cabin if they meet local rules; on other routes, or if the conditions are not met, the dog is carried in the cargo hold in a leak-proof, hard-shell crate with a lock that you provide. Under Japanese airport management rules, dogs other than certified assistance dogs may only be inside airport terminals in a suitable carrier or cage; walking those dogs on the terminal floor is not permitted. ANA: assistance dogs

Official rule On Japan Airlines international flights, an ordinary pet is transported as cargo. Advance reservation is required. French Bulldogs and Bulldogs are not accepted on any JAL flights. Dogs and cats must be at least 8 weeks old; pregnant pets are not accepted. Arrive at least 120 minutes before departure. Charges are given at check-in; the official page does not publish a single yen amount. Assistance dogs (seeing-eye dogs, service dogs, hearing dogs) may accompany the passenger on board at no cost — register the dog when you book. JAL: traveling with pets · JAL: assistance dogs Checked 14 Nov 2026

See also the airline pages ANA and JAL.

Hotels

Official rule A person who uses a certified assistance dog must not be refused at facilities used by an unspecified large number of people, including hotels, except where there is an unavoidable reason under the Act. MHLW states that hotels are among the places where a certified assistance dog may accompany the user. The ministry also states that a business may ask to see the user’s certificate. Ordinary pet policies and any pet fees are set by each hotel — check when you book. MHLW: accepting assistance dogs · MHLW: when you see an assistance dog Checked 14 Nov 2026

Veterinary clinics

AQS does not publish a national directory of clinics on the import pages opened for this guide. For a veterinarian in Japan, ask your hotel, a local government office, or see the vet-info guide.

Grooming

No official national grooming tariff or named salon with dated vaccine rules was opened in this pass. Call ahead and do not treat acceptance of a foreign rabies certificate as confirmed. Also see the Grooming directory.

K9 and assistance dogs

Official rule The Assistance Dogs Act defines a certified assistance dog as a guide dog, a mobility assistance dog or a hearing dog. Public transport operators and facilities used by an unspecified large number of people (MHLW names public facilities, transport, restaurants, supermarkets and hotels) must not refuse the user, except for an unavoidable reason. The user must display the prescribed mark and carry the documents that show the dog does not pose a public-health risk, and must produce them on request. The AQS import pages opened for this guide do not publish a separate working-dog or police-dog waiver of the ordinary health rules. See the taxi, train, flights and hotels sections above. e-Gov — Assistance Dogs Act · AQS · the K9 guide. Checked 14 Nov 2026

FAQ

Can my dog fly in the cabin to Japan as an ordinary pet?

Official rule On ANA-operated international flights and on JAL international flights, an ordinary pet is carried as cargo, not in the cabin. Certified assistance dogs are a separate cabin product, free of charge on the operator pages cited above. Checked 14 Nov 2026

Do I need a rabies titre to enter Japan from the United States?

Official rule If your origin is a non-designated region, yes: a titre of at least 0.5 IU/ml and a 180-day wait after the blood sampling, plus the other AQS steps. Check the current designated-region list on the AQS pages. Checked 14 Nov 2026
