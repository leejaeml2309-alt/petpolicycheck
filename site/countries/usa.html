Pet travel rules for the USA: federal and state requirements

What you need to fly a dog or cat into, out of, or around the United States. Federal rules come from the CDC and USDA; each state sets its own entry rules.

Quick answer

  • Dogs entering the US: CDC Dog Import Form receipt for every dog, plus a healthy look, age 6 months or older and a microchip. Dogs from high-risk rabies countries need extra forms (see below). Source
  • Cats entering the US: No federal rabies proof or USDA health requirements for pet cats; they must look healthy. State, airline, Hawaii and Guam rules still apply. Source
  • Traveling between states: No federal rule: the destination state decides. Many states want a CVI (health certificate, usually issued within 30 days) and proof of rabies vaccination; airlines often want a certificate within 10 days. Source
  • Hawaii: Rabies-free with a quarantine program: two rabies vaccinations, a passing FAVN test and a 30-day wait are needed to qualify for direct release; otherwise up to 120 days of quarantine. Source

Rules change, especially New World screwworm (NWS) restrictions added by many states in 2026. Always confirm with the official source before you travel. Last checked Oct 23, 2026.

🚕 Airport to hotel with your pet

Uber Pet

Pets
One pet per trip: a small animal such as a dog or a cat. Additional pets are at the driver's discretion. No breed or size restrictions.
Fee
Uber Pet costs more than other vehicle options: an Uber Pet surcharge is included in the upfront fare when you select it. A cleaning fee may be charged if your pet leaves waste, excessive hair or damages the vehicle. Uber's driver page says drivers earn an extra Uber Pet fee of $4 in the US ($5 in Canada).
Where
Choose Uber Pet in the vehicle options of the Uber app where it is offered. It is an UberX option served by drivers who opt in (not UberXL or Uber Black); in some areas you can reserve it in advance (reservation fee in the upfront fare).
Tips
Restrain your pet with a leash, harness or crate/carrier, bring a blanket or towel for the seat, and ask the driver where the pet should sit.

Source: Uber Help: Uber Pet (riders) ↗ · Uber Help: Uber Pet (drivers) ↗ · Uber Help: Tolls, surcharges and fees ↗

Lyft Pet

Pets
One well-behaved animal, plus up to four people. As a courtesy, the pet should ride in a carrier, on a blanket or at your feet.
Fee
An additional fee that goes directly to the driver. Lyft's driver page says drivers earn a $4 bonus for every Pet ride.
Where
U.S. only. Select the Pet ride option in the Lyft app.

Source: Lyft Help: Pet rides for riders ↗ · Lyft Help: Pet rides for drivers ↗

  • Tesla Robotaxi: No pets. Disabled riders may bring service animals. Service in limited areas of Miami, Orlando and Tampa (FL) and Austin, Dallas and Houston (TX). Tesla Support: Robotaxi ↗

By train (Amtrak)

  • Who: Dogs and cats only, at least 8 weeks old with vaccinations current; one pet per passenger and one pet per carrier.
  • Weight and carrier: 20 lb max, pet plus carrier combined. Leak-proof, ventilated carrier, max 19 x 14 x 10.5 in, under your own seat; it counts as a carry-on.
  • Trips: Up to 7 hours, including connections. Coach and Acela Business only (not First Class, sleeping cars or food service cars; not the Quiet Car on Keystone and Pennsylvanian).
  • Booking: Reserve a spot for your pet; the fee varies by route. Sign the Pet Release online and check in 45 minutes before departure at staffed stations.
  • No pets: Auto Train, Gold Runner and Amtrak Connection services, or to Canada on the Adirondack, Maple Leaf and Amtrak Cascades.

Service animals are accepted on all trains. Source: Amtrak: Pets on trains ↗, checked Oct 24, 2026.

Pet insurance while traveling in the US

Most US pet insurance policies reimburse care at any licensed vet in the US, and many also in Canada, so an emergency vet on a road trip can be covered. Waiting periods (up to 30 days for illnesses among the 11 insurers we checked) mean a policy bought just before a trip may not cover it yet.
