Pet travel rules for France: EU entry, customs and category-dog bans

France applies the EU pet-entry rules, with extra national rules (documents in French, an adult tooth for dogs from third countries, and a ban on importing or transiting Catégorie 1 dogs). The EU framework in force since 22 April 2026 is Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/131 and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/705. Checked 10 November 2026. Always confirm with the ministry, customs and your airline before you fly.

Every entry rule below is labelled Official rule. Nothing on this page is labelled Field-Verified. The French ministry FAQ page is dated 11 June 2025 and still cites older Regulations (EU) 576/2013 and 577/2013 — we flag that wherever it matters.

Entry rules (Ministry of Agriculture / Douanes)

Authority: French Ministry of Agriculture FAQ — importing pets from third countries (page dated 11 June 2025) and Service-Public F35788 (verified 23 July 2026). EU listed-country table: European Commission listing page (dateModified 7 July 2026).

Dogs

Microchip
Official rule ISO transponder implanted before or on the same day as the rabies vaccination (otherwise re-vaccinate). A tattoo is accepted only if applied before 3 July 2011 and still clearly readable. Source Checked 10 Nov 2026
Rabies
Official rule Inactivated or recombinant vaccine given at 12 weeks of age or older, after identification. Wait at least 21 days after the primary vaccination before entry from a listed country. Source Checked 10 Nov 2026
Rabies blood test (titer)
Official rule Required only from third countries that are not listed in Annex I or Annex II of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/636: sample at least 30 days after vaccination, result at least 0.5 IU/ml from an EU-designated lab, then wait 3 months from the sample date. The United States, Canada, Mexico and Great Britain are listed (no titer). South Korea does not appear on that Commission list. Commission list Checked 10 Nov 2026
Health certificate
Official rule From a non-EU country: an EU animal health certificate — current model in Annex III to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/705 — issued by an official veterinarian or issued by an authorised veterinarian and endorsed by the competent authority, plus a written non-commercial declaration. Valid 10 days from issue until the checks at the travellers’ point of entry (extended by the length of a sea journey). For later travel to other EU countries the Commission states validity of 6 months from those checks or until the rabies vaccination expires, whichever is earlier. EU residents use an EU pet passport. Pet passports issued under the previous model remain valid; old-model passports issued before 1 January 2028 and animal health certificates issued before 1 October 2026 (usable until 31 March 2027) are deemed to comply (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/131, Art. 31; Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/705, Art. 6). Commission · (EU) 2026/131 Checked 10 Nov 2026
Language, numbers, arrival
Official rule Documents must be in French (or bilingual) or translated by a sworn translator. No import permit and no customs appointment — present yourself spontaneously to customs. Non-commercial movement: maximum 5 pets (the Commission/BMLEH wording is a maximum of 5 pet animals in a single vehicle). Ministry FAQ Checked 10 Nov 2026
Adult tooth / age
Official rule A dog imported from a third country may enter only if it has at least one adult tooth (Code rural L236-1). Minimum practical age from a listed country is about 15 weeks (12 weeks + 21 days). From a titer country the ministry FAQ example is about 198 days (12 weeks + 30 days + 3 months). Ministry FAQ · F35788 Checked 10 Nov 2026
  • Official rule After arrival, dogs and cats must be registered with Icad within 7 days. Service-Public F35788 (verified 23 July 2026) describes a provisional identification form and a €12 cheque to I-Cad. Source
  • Official rule If the rules are not met, officers may order return, quarantine or euthanasia at the owner’s cost (Code rural L236-9 and L236-10). Source
  • Service-Public F35788 also mentions a clinical examination 48 hours before travel and completing section 10 of the pet passport. The ministry FAQ for third-country imports does not state that as a separate extra step beyond issuing the official certificate.

Cats

Microchip, rabies, documents
Official rule Same identification, rabies, listed/unlisted titer and certificate/passport rules as for dogs. Service-Public F35788 states that any cat (pedigree or mixed) may be brought into France. Checked 10 Nov 2026
  • Official rule Bengal or Savannah cats of generations F1–F4 are treated as non-domestic (CITES / special permit). F5 and above with a recognised pedigree are treated as domestic cats. Ministry FAQ

Arriving from the USA, South Korea or the EU

From the United States

  • Official rule The United States is listed in Annex II of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/636 — no rabies titer. Source Checked 10 Nov 2026
  • Official rule USDA APHIS France page: USDA-accredited veterinarian issues the EU non-commercial certificate; APHIS original ink endorsement is required; endorsement must fall within 10 days of arrival in the EU. New non-commercial certificates took effect 1 October 2026 (EU 2026/131 and related implementing acts). Primary US rabies vaccination is treated as valid for 1 year for EU purposes even if a 3-year product was used — keep boosters without gaps. USDA APHIS dateModified 1 Sep 2026; checked 10 Nov 2026
  • See also the route page USA ↔ France.

From South Korea

Official rule The Republic of Korea (South Korea) does not appear in Annex I or Annex II on the Commission listing page fetched on 10 November 2026. Pets coming from Korea therefore need a rabies antibody titration test (sample at least 30 days after vaccination, result at least 0.5 IU/ml at an EU-designated lab) and a 3-month wait from the sample date, plus the EU animal health certificate and declaration. The French ministry FAQ list dated 11 June 2025 also does not name Corée / République de Corée.

Official rule Before you leave South Korea, apply to the Animal and Plant Quarantine Agency (APQA/QIA) for export quarantine. For export, the official application page asks for a vaccination certificate and a veterinary document stating that the animal is healthy and there is no risk of spreading a livestock infectious disease, plus any extra papers the destination country requires. The stated fee for a dog or cat is 10,000 won per application. You may apply in person, by post, by fax or online; the stated processing time is 1 day. QIA publishes country-specific export guidance, including a sheet for the EU — check that sheet, because foreign rules can change without notice. Some regional offices require a reservation at least one day ahead. Use the export-pet quarantine reservation system (QIA announced a move to eminwon.apqa.go.kr/reservation from 23 October 2026). QIA: country-specific pet export guidance · QIA e-minwon: animal quarantine application Checked 10 Nov 2026

From another EU country

  • Official rule From an EU member state: at least 15 weeks old, identified, rabies-vaccinated (primary vaccine at 12 weeks or older, valid 3 weeks after the first injection), and accompanied by a valid EU pet passport. F35788 Verified 23 July 2026; checked 10 Nov 2026

Where you enter

  • Official rule From a third country the pet must pass through a travellers’ point of entry. Present yourself spontaneously to customs with the original documents. Do not send papers in advance and do not book a customs appointment. Ministry FAQ
  • Official rule Transit through France still counts as entry into the EU: the animal must meet EU rules. Transit of a Catégorie 1 dog through France is prohibited. Source

Catégorie 1 and Catégorie 2 dogs

Official rule Catégorie 1 (“chiens d’attaque”: non-LOF dogs of morphotype American Staffordshire Terrier / “pit-bulls”, Mastiff / “boerbulls”, or Tosa): import and transit into France are prohibited (Code rural L211-15 / L215-2). Buying, selling, giving or introducing a Catégorie 1 dog is an offence (the ministry page dated 23 July 2026 cites 6 months’ imprisonment and a €15,000 fine). Ministry — category dogs · Service-Public F1839 (now the category-dog page, verified 7 November 2025) · Douane PDF (July 2026)

Official rule Catégorie 2 (LOF-registered American Staffordshire Terrier, Rottweiler or Tosa, plus Rottweiler-type dogs without a pedigree): import is allowed if the owner holds the French Catégorie 2 paperwork (permis de détention and related duties). Staffordshire Bull Terriers (“Staffies”) are a different breed and are not a category dog. Look-alike is not automatic — a veterinarian decides the morphotype. The ministry recommends a signed attestation, translated into French if needed, stating the dog is not in the 27 April 1999 annex categories, which officers may still re-examine.

Return to the United States

Official rule CDC: every dog entering the US needs a CDC Dog Import Form receipt, age 6 months or older, a microchip, and a healthy appearance. France is not on the CDC high-risk dog-rabies list if the dog has not been in a high-risk country in the past 6 months. Cats: appear healthy at federal level; state and airline rules still apply. An EU pet passport does not replace the CDC Dog Import Form for dogs. CDC · APHIS cats Checked 10 Nov 2026

A mandatory French/EU exit certificate solely for return to the US is not stated as required on the APHIS France and CDC pages reviewed — confirm with your airline and a French veterinarian if asked.

Taxi and private hire (VTC)

Uber Pet (France)

Official rule Uber publishes a France rider page for Uber Pet. Where the option appears in the app, you can request a pet-friendly ride. Uber says you can travel with up to 3 passengers and 1 animal, and that you can bring one pet per trip, such as a dog or a cat; availability may vary by location. Use a lead, harness or carrier for a dog or cat; other pets should travel in a carrier. Uber says journeys may include an additional fee compared with other ride options, and a cleaning fee may apply if the animal leaves waste, excessive hair or causes damage. The rider page does not publish a fixed extra amount — check the upfront price in the app. (Driver-facing extras are not used as the rider surcharge.) You do not need to select Uber Pet when travelling with a service animal; you can request any ride option. Uber Pet (France) Checked 10 Nov 2026

Official rule Service-Public F35787 (verified 13 March 2026; fetched 10 November 2026): guide dogs and assistance dogs that accompany a person holding a carte mobilité inclusion (CMI) with the “invalidité” and “priorité” mentions are admitted in shops, and more generally in all places open to the public, in transports, and in workplaces, training places and schools. The same applies when the dog is with the person responsible for its education during training. The presence of the dog must not lead to any extra charge (the page gives trains as an example). Refusing access to places open to the public is punishable by a fine of up to €450 for a natural person and €2,250 for a legal person (law n° 87-588 of 30 July 1987, article 88; Criminal Code article R241-23). The page does not create a duty for ordinary taxis to accept a pet dog or cat. Service-Public F35787 Vérifié le 13 mars 2026; checked 10 Nov 2026

Bus, metro, RER and tram (RATP)

Official rule RATP page “Voyager avec un animal” (modified 28 August 2026; fetched 10 November 2026): small animals (the page names cats, small dogs, rabbits and rodents) travel free on the whole network in a closed bag, basket or crate adapted to their size. Larger dogs are accepted only on the metro and RER (not on buses or trams), free of charge, and must be muzzled and on a lead. Assistance and guide dogs are admitted everywhere free of charge, without a muzzle, on a lead and wearing an identification harness; an agent may ask for proof. Category 1 and category 2 dogs are not admitted (this can lead to a fine). An animal outside a suitable container, or a large dog without a muzzle, can also expose you to a fine. RATP: voyager avec un animal Modifié le 28 août 2026; checked 10 Nov 2026

Train (SNCF and Eurostar)

Official rule TGV INOUI (national): a small animal travels in a closed bag or basket no larger than 45 × 30 × 25 cm, on your lap or at your feet; a larger dog must be leashed and muzzled and stay at your feet. The TGV INOUI page states a national fare of €10 per TGV INOUI. SNCF Connect says “à partir de 10€” (from €10) for each train used, and a maximum of two animals per traveller. INTERCITÉS de nuit: €19 in seated places; pets are not allowed in couchette cars except assistance dogs. OUIGO: €10 regardless of weight. Pets are not accepted on Eurostar services to England except assistance dogs. TGV INOUI · SNCF Connect Checked 10 Nov 2026

A 6 kg weight limit is not stated on the SNCF Connect or TGV INOUI pages fetched on 10 November 2026 — it is not published here as an Official rule. (Eurostar does publish its own 6 kg / 45 × 30 × 25 cm cabin-style rule on permitted continental routes — that is an Eurostar figure, not an SNCF domestic figure.)

Official rule Eurostar (operator page, checked 10 November 2026), which is stricter than a general SNCF domestic rule: pets are not allowed on trains to and from London, on trains between Brussels or the Netherlands and Lille, or on Eurostar Sun and Snow. Pets are welcome on other Eurostar routes between Belgium, France, Germany and the Netherlands. On those permitted routes, small domestic animals under 6 kg travel free in a carrier no larger than 45 × 30 × 25 cm; larger dogs need a “dog” fare at a single price of €30, bought from the Train Manager, and must be leashed and muzzled. Guide and assistance dogs and cats always travel free. For assistance animals on London trains, contact Eurostar at least 24 hours ahead (no charge; maximum four assistance animals per train; arrive 60 minutes early). Dangerous dogs on the UK banned-type list and French category 1 and 2 dogs are refused on all Eurostar services. Eurostar: travelling with pets · Eurostar: assistance dogs and cats

Flights — French airlines and France entry

Airline cabin and hold rules are separate from France’s entry rules (chip, rabies, listed/unlisted titer, EU certificate or passport, documents in French, adult tooth for dogs from third countries, Catégorie 1 import and transit ban). A pet accepted by the airline can still be refused at the border.

Air France

Official rule If the cat or dog plus its transport crate weighs more than 8 kg and up to 75 kg, it must travel in the hold. Add the animal to the reservation no later than 24 hours before departure (subject to space and hold conditions). Less than 24 hours before departure, if the airport customer-service team still accepts the animal, a surcharge applies: 50% on flights in metropolitan France and intra-Caribbean, 25% on international flights. For dogs, Air France also asks you to request the reservation 48 hours before departure (96 hours for assistance dogs to or from the United Kingdom and the United Arab Emirates). Inside the EU the animal must be microchipped and have an EU pet passport; extra health rules apply for Ireland, Sweden, the United Kingdom or Malta. Animals should stay in their transport bag in Air France lounges; assistance dogs are exempt but must be on a lead and kept close to you. Air France: voyager avec son animal Checked 10 Nov 2026

Official rule Official cabin checklist (Air France / KLM document marked October 2025, fetched 10 November 2026): total weight of the animal and the bag must not exceed 8 kg; the bag must not exceed L46 × W28 × H24 cm; the animal must be able to stand, turn and lie down; the bag must be closed, ventilated and waterproof; the animal must stay inside from boarding until disembarkation. Only soft transport bags are accepted on Air France-operated flights (KLM also accepts crates or wicker baskets). Air France cabin checklist (PDF, October 2025). Policy details coming soon. See also Air France pet policy (last verified 22 October 2026).

Transavia

Official rule Cabin: dogs and cats in a flexible (not hard-plastic) official pet carrier, maximum 40 × 30 × 24 cm, maximum 8 kg including the bag, minimum age 15 weeks. The animal must stay in the closed carrier under the seat; you may not open or move it during the flight. If two people in your party each travel with a pet, they cannot sit in the same row. Besides the pet you may also bring one piece of hand luggage. Emotional support animals and small rodents (hamsters, rabbits, guinea pigs, and similar) are not accepted. Price published on the operator page: if the flight number starts with HV, €50 per pet per one-way flight; if it starts with TO, between €60 and €180 per pet per one-way flight. Transavia: pet in the cabin Checked 10 Nov 2026

Official rule Hold kennel: maximum 102 × 69 × 76 cm; maximum 75 kg including the pet; IATA-style hard kennel rules (fibreglass or hard plastic; no wooden, metal-bar or welded-wire kennels; central locking; and further construction details on the page). Policy details coming soon. Transavia: pets in the hold

Hotels

Official rule Accor publishes official pet-friendly hotel listings for France. The ibis / ibis Styles / ibis budget page (fetched 10 November 2026) welcomes dogs and cats in the room and in common areas (on a leash and supervised in common areas; not on furniture; not in kitchen areas) and states that a “small fee [is] charged per night”, with amenities and the fee subject to each hotel’s policy. It does not publish one group-wide euro amount. Accor: pet-friendly hotels in France · ibis pet-friendly hotels Checked 10 Nov 2026

Parks and dog spaces

Official rule The City of Paris page “Les chiens à Paris” (updated 2 September 2026; fetched 10 November 2026) says leashed pets may enter 165 green spaces that are signed as dog-accessible. On those sites they may use the paths (allées) and must stay off lawns and planted beds. In December 2025 the city added further spaces, including Parc Monceau (8th), Square de la place Pasdeloup (11th) and Parc de Bercy (12th). There are 45 dedicated dog areas (espaces canins) where dogs may be off the lead. Larger off-lead areas of more than one hectare exist in the Bois de Boulogne and the Bois de Vincennes. Dogs are not allowed on the Petite Ceinture. In public space a dog must be kept on a lead. You must pick up dog waste; the stated fine for not doing so is €135. Dogs are forbidden in cemeteries, except guide and assistance dogs (including dogs in training), which are allowed everywhere. paris.fr: Les chiens à Paris Mise à jour le 02/09/2026; checked 10 Nov 2026

FAQ

Do I need a rabies titer for France from the US?

Official rule No. The United States is listed. South Korea is not listed, so a titer and a 3-month wait apply from Korea.

Which certificate model should I use in 2026?

Official rule The current EU model is Annex III of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/705 (in force since 22 April 2026; APHIS required the new non-commercial form from 1 October 2026). The French ministry FAQ dated 11 June 2025 still points to the old Annex IV model of Regulation (EU) 577/2013. Follow the current EU/APHIS model.

Can I transit France with a pit-bull-type dog?

Official rule No, if the dog is Catégorie 1. Import and transit are both prohibited.

What happened to Service-Public F1839?

F1839 now explains the rules for owning a category dog (verified 7 November 2025). The current Service-Public page for bringing a dog or cat back from abroad is F35788 (verified 23 July 2026).
