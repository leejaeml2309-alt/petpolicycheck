Pet travel rules for India: AQCS import of dogs and cats

National rules from the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Animal Quarantine and Certification Services (AQCS), and the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC). Checked 28 November 2026. Confirm with AQCS and your airline before you travel.

Authority: AQCS — Import of Pets under Baggage, dog SOP, and cat SOP. Customs facilitation: CBIC Circular No. 07/2025-Customs (5 March 2025).

Entry rules

Only dogs and cats may enter as pets under the baggage rules, and only up to two per passenger, if you are transferring your residence to India after two years of continuous stay abroad. Commercial breeding imports are not permitted.

Baggage route (transfer of residence)

  1. At least seven days before departure (the SOP flowcharts say seven working days), apply on CBIC’s ATITHI 2.0 portal for an Advance No Objection Certificate (NOC). Upload the vaccination and microchip certificate, the official health certificate, the ticket or airway bill, and a passport copy. The processing charge is Rs 1,000, paid through Bharatkosh. Airlines must not board the pet without a valid Advance NOC. A fake or altered NOC can lead to deportation or re-export.
  2. The dog or cat must be vaccinated against rabies more than one month, but within 12 months, before embarkation. A dog must be more than three months old at the time of that vaccination. Get an official health certificate from the country of origin. For cats, the SOP requires a physical examination within seven days of shipment.
  3. Enter only through a notified port: the airports at Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Kochi; and the seaports or airports at Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata. The AQCS FAQ still lists six stations and does not name Kochi; confirm the port with AQCS.
  4. On arrival, AQCS examines the pet at the port. If the animal is sound and healthy, the final NOC is issued at the port, around the clock. If the examination fails, the pet is quarantined for 15 days, and the owner pays for testing.
  5. An unaccompanied pet may arrive within one month after the owner, or earlier or later with advance Customs permission.

Other routes and recent changes

If you are not transferring residence

Apply first for a Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) license, then for an AQCS NOC. Re-import of a pet previously taken out of India is a separate path and needs proof of earlier Indian certification, preferably from AQCS.

Recent official changes

  • 10 October 2024: Kochi International Airport was added to the notified ports (cited in Circular 07/2025).
  • 20 February 2025 and 5 March 2025: the final NOC is issued at the port of entry, around the clock, if the owner already has an Advance NOC and has emailed the arrival date and time.
  • 2 February 2026: the Baggage Rules, 2026, entered into force. Pet import remains governed by the rules notified by the Central Government (DAHD and AQCS).

Some older AQCS FAQ and baggage-page texts still describe provisional clearance and a 30-day final clearance. The 2025 SOP and Circular 07/2025 provide for a final NOC at the port if the pet is healthy.

Airlines

Figures are from each airline’s official pet page, checked 28 November 2026. Confirm the operating carrier before you book. Vistara’s website redirects to Air India; use Air India’s rules.

IndiGo

  • Cabin: no for ordinary pets. Guide dogs for blind customers are exempt and must be booked through the IndiGo Call Centre.
How to book↗

Akasa Air

  • Domestic flights within India only.
  • Cabin: yes, up to 10 kg. Hold: yes, up to 32 kg at check-in, or up to 100 kg from the cargo terminal.
  • Pets must be at least 3 months old; book at least 24 hours ahead.
Pets on Akasa↗

Air Canada

  • Cabin: yes, for a cat or small dog in a soft-sided carrier under the seat.
  • Hold: no to India from 1 March to 31 October.
Travelling with your Pet↗

Airlines (continued)

Lufthansa

  • Cabin: yes, dogs and cats, pet plus container 8 kg or less.
  • Hold: yes, as additional baggage.
  • Restriction: Lufthansa may refuse bookings on certain routes, including Bangalore.
Animals as additional carry-on baggage↗

Etihad

  • Cabin: yes, cats and dogs at least 16 weeks old, pet plus carrier 8 kg or less. Book at least seven days ahead.
  • Restriction: pets are not permitted to or from Ahmedabad, Kozhikode, Jaipur, or Thiruvananthapuram. Named breeds, including pit bull types, mastiffs, Rottweiler, Doberman, and Boxer, are not permitted.
Travelling with pets↗

Singapore Airlines

  • Cabin: no — ordinary pets travel as checked baggage only.
  • Hold: yes — dogs and cats at least 3 months old; if pet plus container exceeds 32 kg, use a cargo agent. Not accepted on Boeing 737-operated flights.
  • Ban: named brachycephalic dog and cat breeds, including shih tzu and crossbreeds, are not accepted as checked baggage.
Travelling with pets↗

Air India

  • Cabin: yes, dogs and cats, pet plus carrier 10 kg (22 lb) or less. Book at least 48 hours ahead. The health and rabies certificate must not be older than 7 days.
  • Hold: yes as checked baggage from 10 kg up to 32 kg (70 lb); over 32 kg, cargo. Cargo is not accepted on ultra long-haul flights (over 12 hours) to the US and Canada.
  • Restriction: the passenger FAQ does not allow snub-nosed breeds in the cargo compartment; the cargo page allows them only when the temperature is below 25°C. Pets in the cabin are not permitted to the United States, Canada, Australia, or the United Kingdom, or on flights from India to the UAE.
Pet Travel FAQs↗

Airlines (continued)

Emirates

  • Cabin: no, except falcons on certain Dubai–Pakistan flights and guide dogs.
  • Hold: yes if the itinerary starts in Dubai, the destination allows animals as excess baggage, and total journey time is under 17 hours (from USD 500 when animal plus cage do not exceed 23 kg and 150 cm).
  • Cargo: required if the itinerary ends in Dubai, exceeds 17 hours, or the destination does not allow animals as check-in baggage.
Rules and charges↗

Qatar Airways

  • Cabin: no for ordinary pets (falcons and service dogs only).
  • Hold: yes, up to 75 kg — USD 450 / CAD 590 on routes other than to or from Qatar (USD 300 / CAD 390 to or from Qatar). Over 75 kg or 300 cm, cargo.
Travelling with pets↗

United

  • Cabin: yes, cats and dogs, $150 each way. India is not on United’s embargo list.
  • Hold: no. Cargo is only for certain U.S. military or State Department travelers.
Traveling with pets↗

Air France

  • Cabin: yes if the pet plus carrier is 8 kg (17.64 lb) or less.
  • Hold: yes, over 8 kg and up to 75 kg (165.35 lb). Add the pet at least 24 hours before departure.

Hotels

Info only. Official hotel URLs are in Sources, not as booking links. Policies change; confirm with the hotel before you travel.

  • New Delhi — JW Marriott Hotel New Delhi Aerocity: pets in guest rooms only; muzzle required in public areas; max 2; 35 kg; INR 5,000.
  • Mumbai — Lakeside Chalet, Mumbai — Marriott Executive Apartments: max 2 pets.
  • Bengaluru — Bengaluru Marriott Hotel Whitefield: max 2; the official page lists ₹1,200 per stay and ₹600 per night.
