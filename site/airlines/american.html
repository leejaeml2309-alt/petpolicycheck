American Airlines pet policy

✓ Last verified Oct 22, 2026 against the official American Airlines pet policy page.

Pets in cabinYes
Species acceptedCats and dogs
Max carrier sizeSoft-sided (recommended): 18 x 11 x 11 in (46 x 28 x 28 cm); hard-sided mainline: 19 x 13 x 9 in; hard-sided American Eagle: 16 x 12 x 8 in
Max weight (pet + carrier)20 lb (9.07 kg) pet + carrier on A321T premium cabins; checked kennel max 100 lb (45 kg) combined. No general in-cabin limit stated.
Fees
  • Carry-on: U.S., Canada, Mexico, Central America, Caribbean: $150 per kennel each way
  • Checked (active-duty military / State Dept only): $200 per kennel; $150 to/from Brazil
  • Cargo (PetEmbark): Varies
Checked bag / cargo optionChecked pets only for active-duty U.S. Military and State Department Foreign Service on official orders. Others: American Airlines Cargo (PetEmbark).
Breed restrictionsChecked: brachycephalic cats (e.g. Burmese, Persian, Himalayan, Exotic Shorthair) and brachycephalic dogs (e.g. Boxer, Bulldog, Pug, Shih Tzu, Pit Bull, Mastiff, and more).
Route restrictionsNo carry-on pets to/from Hawaii, Jamaica, Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Uruguay, or on trans-Atlantic and trans-Pacific trips.
Service animalsFully-trained service dogs fly in the cabin at no charge if they meet requirements.
