Delta Air Lines pet policy

✓ Last verified Oct 22, 2026 against the official Delta Air Lines pet policy page.

Pets in cabinYes
Species acceptedSmall dogs, cats and household birds (birds only within the contiguous U.S.)
Max carrier sizeDepends on aircraft under-seat space; Delta recommends a soft-sided kennel of max 18 x 11 x 11 in, which fits most aircraft
Max weight (pet + carrier)Check with airline
Fees
  • U.S./Canada/Puerto Rico/U.S. Virgin Islands: $150 USD/CAD each way (tickets issued on/after Apr 8, 2025; $95 before)
  • International: $200 USD/CAD/EUR each way
  • Brazil: $200 USD each way (tickets issued on/after Apr 8, 2025; $75 before)
Checked bag / cargo optionNo checked pets for the general public. Delta Cargo ships pets only for active U.S. Military or U.S. State Department Foreign Service personnel with permanent change of station orders (until further notice).
Breed restrictionsBrachycephalic (snub-nosed) dogs, cats and their mixes are not permitted in cargo (per Delta's Shipping Your Pet page).
Route restrictionsNo in-cabin pets to/from Australia, Barbados, Brazil/Colombia/Dominican Republic (originating), Hawaii (flights to), Hong Kong, Iceland, Jamaica, New Zealand, South Africa, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates. No pets in Delta One, Delta Premium or international Business.
Service animalsTrained service animals follow separate guidelines (see Delta's service animal page).
