United Airlines pet policy

✓ Last verified Oct 22, 2026 against the official United Airlines pet policy page.

Pets in cabinYes
Species acceptedCats and dogs only
Max carrier sizeHard-sided: 9 x 17.5 x 12 in (22 x 44 x 30 cm); soft-sided: 11 x 18 x 11 in (27 x 45 x 27 cm); max 10 in tall on Boeing 737 MAX
Max weight (pet + carrier)No weight limit (United states there are no weight or breed limitations; pet must fit the carrier)
Fees
  • All routes: $150 each way
Checked bag / cargo optionPetSafe checked-pet program ended. Pets fly in cargo only with certain active-duty military or State Department employees.
Breed restrictionsNone for in-cabin pets (United states no breed limitations).
Route restrictionsNo pets to/from/through: Australia, Barbados, Brazil, Cuba, Dominica, Dominican Republic, French Polynesia, Greenland, Guam (partial), Guyana, Hawaii, Hong Kong, Iceland, Ireland, Jamaica, Marshall Islands, Micronesia, Mongolia, Morocco, New Zealand, Northern Mariana Islands, Palau, Philippines, St Kitts & Nevis, South Africa, Spain (Bilbao only), Sweden, Taiwan (partial), Thailand, Trinidad & Tobago, UAE, United Kingdom, Vietnam.
Service animalsTherapy and emotional support cats and dogs must fly as pets, not service animals.

Good to know

Up to 2 pets per person on most flights (second requires an adjacent purchased seat); only 1 per person on A319, A320, A321neo, 737-800/8, 737-900/9.
