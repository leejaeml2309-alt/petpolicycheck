Snub-nosed / brachycephalic breeds

Compare official cabin and hold/cargo rules for short-nosed dogs and cats across 12 airlines: Snub-nosed breeds airline rules (Official rule).

Pets in cabinYes
Species acceptedCats and small dogs (one per passenger, at least 10 weeks old)
Max carrier sizeSoft-sided only. Most mainline jets: 43 x 40 x 20 cm (17 x 16 x 8 in) L x W x H; Dash 8-400/E175/CRJ900: 55 x 40 x 27 cm; widebody Business Class: 41 x 21 x 28 cm
Max weight (pet + carrier)Baggage compartment: up to 45 kg (100 lb) pet + kennel; max 292 cm (115 in) linear
Fees
  • Cabin: within Canada and Canada/U.S.: CA/US $50-$60 one way
  • Cabin: international: CA/US $100-$120 one way
  • Baggage compartment: within Canada and Canada/U.S.: CA/US $105-$126 one way
  • Baggage compartment: international: CA/US $270-$324 one way
Checked bag / cargo optionYes, in the baggage compartment (hard-sided kennel) with seasonal and route embargoes; Air Canada Cargo (AC Animals) for unaccompanied pets.
Breed restrictionsShort/snub-nosed (brachycephalic) dog breeds cannot travel in the baggage compartment; 'strong' breeds need reinforced crates.
Route restrictionsNo pet carriers on Air Canada Jetz or codeshare flights operated by partners; no cabin pets in Premium Economy. Many seasonal baggage-compartment embargoes (e.g. Dec 18 - Jan 4 no checked pets or cargo).
Service animalsSee Air Canada's accessibility services page for service or emotional support dogs.

Good to know

Register your pet with Air Canada Reservations, ideally within 24 hours of booking; online/kiosk check-in not available with a pet.
