Alaska Airlines (and Hawaiian Airlines) pet policy

✓ Last verified Oct 22, 2026 against the official Alaska Airlines (and Hawaiian Airlines) pet policy page.

Pets in cabinYes
Species acceptedCheck with airline
Max carrier sizeSoft-sided carrier max 17 x 11 x 9.5 in (per Alaska's official newsroom, Aug 26, 2026)
Max weight (pet + carrier)Baggage compartment: up to 150 lb pet + kennel combined (official newsroom, Aug 26, 2026)
Fees
  • In cabin: $100 per carrier each way ($35 wholly within Hawaii)
  • Baggage compartment: $200 per kennel each way (discounts within Alaska/Hawaii and for active-duty military)
Checked bag / cargo optionYes: temperature-controlled baggage compartment on your flight, or Pet Connect via Alaska Air Cargo for unaccompanied pets.
Breed restrictionsBrachycephalic (short-nosed) dogs are not permitted in the baggage compartment (official newsroom).
Route restrictionsCheck with airline
Service animalsTrained service animals travel free; see Alaska's accessible travel services section.
