British Airways pet policy

✓ Last verified Oct 22, 2026 against the official British Airways pet policy page.

Pets in cabinNo
Species acceptedNone (no pets in the cabin on any route)
Max carrier sizeNot applicable: BA carries no pets in the cabin
Max weight (pet + carrier)Check with airline
FeesCheck with airline
Checked bag / cargo optionPets travel in the hold, arranged by partners: PetAir UK (from the UK) or IAG Cargo (to the UK).
Breed restrictionsCheck with airline
Route restrictionsCheck with airline
Service animalsAssistance dogs fly free in the cabin on most BA flights; emotional support dogs are not accepted in the cabin and must travel as pets.
