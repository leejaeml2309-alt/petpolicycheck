Finnair pet policy

✓ Last verified Nov 18, 2026 against the official Finnair pets page.

Pets in cabinYes
Species acceptedCats and dogs may travel in the cabin or the hold from EU and non-EU countries. Pet rabbits, tortoises and hedgehogs are allowed in the cabin or the hold only from EU countries. Ferrets may travel in the hold from EU and non-EU countries. All other species are forbidden on board and can only be shipped as cargo.
Max carrier sizeCabin, on Finnair- and Norra-operated flights: soft carriers 55 × 40 × 23 cm (21.7 × 15.7 × 9.1 in), with 23 cm the maximum compressed height; hard carriers 35 × 30 × 20 cm (14.8 × 11.9 × 7.9 in). Hold crate maxima (L × W × H): 122 × 81 × 89 cm on Airbus A319, A320, A321, A330 and A350; 101 × 68 × 76 cm on Embraer E90 (Norra); 81 × 57 × 53 cm on ATR (Norra).
Max weight (pet + carrier)Cabin: maximum total weight of the carrier and the pet is 8 kg (17.6 lb). Hold: 75 kg (165 lb) on Finnair-operated flights; 50 kg (110 lb) on Norra-operated flights. Above those hold limits, the pet must be shipped as cargo.
Fees
  • Fees are per pet carrier or container, per direction, for flights operated by Finnair (AY code on the ticket). When departing from India, local taxes are added.
  • Cabin, Finland/Scandinavia/Baltics: EUR 60 or 7,800 Avios (7 days or more before departure); EUR 65 or 8,500 Avios (6 days or less).
  • Cabin, within Europe: EUR 70 or 9,100 Avios (7 days or more); EUR 80 or 10,500 Avios (6 days or less).
  • Cabin, intercontinental (including the Middle East, excluding Australia): EUR 140 / USD 160 or 18,500 Avios (7 days or more); EUR 170 / USD 190 or 22,500 Avios (6 days or less).
  • Hold, Finland/Scandinavia/Baltics: EUR 140 or 18,500 Avios.
  • Hold, within Europe: EUR 160 or 21,100 Avios.
  • Hold, intercontinental: EUR 650 / USD 750 or 86,500 Avios.
  • Cargo pricing is on the Finnair Cargo website.
Checked bag / cargo optionYes: bigger dogs and ferrets may travel in the hold on the same flight if weight and crate limits are met. Cargo is required when the combined weight exceeds the hold limit, for species other than dogs, cats, pet rabbits, tortoises, hedgehogs and ferrets, when travelling from outside the EU (except dogs, cats and ferrets), when travelling to or via certain countries, and for unaccompanied animals and animals brought by a third person from outside the EU.
Route restrictionsWhen travelling to or via certain countries, including for example Great Britain, Hong Kong and the United Arab Emirates, pets can only be shipped as cargo. Finnair does not transport pets to Australia. Pets cannot travel in Business Class on long-haul flights, because there is not enough room under those seats. Hold pets are not possible if the ticket includes flights operated by airlines other than Finnair or Norra. The minimum transfer time between flights with a pet in the hold is 3 hours; hold pets do not transfer automatically to connecting flights.
Service animalsAssistance dogs and working dogs on an official search or rescue mission are welcome in the cabin on all Finnair flights free of charge, regardless of size. Inform Finnair in advance with the special assistance form, at least 48 hours before departure. Working dogs in training are transported in the cargo hold like pets.

Good to know

Cabin pets can be added when booking, or later in Manage booking, but must be added before check-in begins. Finnair can normally accept two customers per flight with pets in the cabin (this may change with aircraft type, destination weather or allergic passengers), and a maximum of one pet carrier per customer. In one cabin carrier you may carry a maximum of two pets if the total weight does not exceed 8 kg. For Business Class on short-haul flights within Europe, or Premium Economy on long-haul flights, contact customer service to add a cabin pet. For hold travel, book the flight online at least 7 days in advance and inform Finnair about the pet within 24 hours via chat, with crate dimensions and the total weight; Finnair aims to confirm within 2 days, or at the latest 2 weeks before departure for the USA. Finnair can usually accept two pets in the hold on Finnair-operated flights and one on Norra-operated flights. Carrier-size requirements may differ on flights operated by Jettime or Iberia; check those airlines' official pet guidelines.

Before you book, re-check the official policy official page and confirm the current rules, space and fees with the airline; space is usually limited per flight.
