Lufthansa pet policy

✓ Last verified Oct 22, 2026 against the official Lufthansa pet policy page.

Pets in cabinYes
Species acceptedSmall dogs and cats only
Max carrier sizeSoft-sided, 55 x 40 x 23 cm (22 x 16 x 9 in), 118 cm total
Max weight (pet + carrier)8 kg pet + carrier in cabin
Fees
  • Cabin: domestic: EUR 65-85 / USD 80-100
  • Cabin: Europe: EUR 80-100 / USD 95-115
  • Cabin: Levant and Egypt: EUR 100-120 / USD 110-130
  • Cabin: intercontinental short haul: EUR 105-125 / USD 120-140
  • Cabin: intercontinental medium haul: EUR 115-135 / USD 135-155
  • Cabin: intercontinental long haul: EUR 125-145 / USD 145-165
Checked bag / cargo optionYes: dogs and cats over 8 kg as excess baggage in the air-conditioned hold; other animals via Lufthansa Cargo.
Breed restrictionsSpecial rules for 'fighting dog' breeds and snub-nosed breeds (see Lufthansa's dedicated pages). Pregnant animals not accepted.
Route restrictionsNew bookings for dogs to the USA only to Atlanta, Los Angeles, Miami, New York-JFK, Philadelphia, Washington DC, and Newark (if no high-risk rabies country in prior 6 months). Bookings may be refused on routes such as the UK, Ireland or Bangalore.
Service animalsAssistance dogs fly in the cabin free of charge; CDC requirements apply for the USA.

Carriers that fit this airline's limits

  • Cabin (55 × 40 × 23 cm): Sherpa Original Deluxe Pet Carrier Small

Based on manufacturer dimensions vs. the limit above; not a guarantee. All airlines · Soft carrier guide

Good to know

Minimum age 12 weeks (15 weeks to/from/via Germany; 6 months for dogs to the USA). Register at least 72 hours before departure (USA flights: online form only).

Recent changes

  • Aug 1, 2024 – After CDC dog-entry rule changes, Lufthansa accepts new dog bookings to the USA only to ATL, LAX, MIA, JFK, PHL and Washington DC. source
  • Oct 1, 2024 – Dogs to Newark (EWR) possible again if not in a high-risk rabies area in the prior 6 months. source

Before you book, re-check the official page and call the airline to reserve pet space; space is usually limited per flight.
