Privacy policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

PetPolicyCheck is a simple static website. This page explains, in plain English, what data is and isn't collected when you visit.

No accounts, no forms

There are no user accounts, logins, comment forms or newsletters on this site. We don't ask for, and don't store, your name, email or any other personal information. If you email us, we only use your message and address to reply.

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you load a page, GitHub's servers may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, for security and operations. We don't receive or control those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Analytics

We currently use no analytics, tracking pixels or advertising scripts, and the site itself sets no cookies.

Update note: if we add an analytics tool in the future, this section will be updated before it goes live to name the tool, what it collects, and how to opt out, and the effective date above will change.

Affiliate links and partner cookies

Some pages may include affiliate links to partners, for example brands on the Impact network (such as Chewy, Trupanion or Lemonade) and Booking.com. If you click one, you leave our site and the partner (or its affiliate network) may set cookies or use similar technology to record that the visit came from us, so we can be paid a commission if you buy something. Those cookies are governed by the partner's own privacy policy, not ours. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. We don't receive personal information about you from these partners, only aggregated sales or commission reports.

External links

We link to airline and government websites. Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
