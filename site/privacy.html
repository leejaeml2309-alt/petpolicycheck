Privacy policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026

PetPolicyCheck is a simple static website. This page explains, in plain English, what data is and isn't collected when you visit, including by the advertising and affiliate services we use.

No accounts, no forms

There are no user accounts, logins, comment forms or newsletters on this site. We don't ask for, and don't store, your name, email or any other personal information. If you email us, we only use your message and address to reply.

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you load a page, GitHub's servers may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, for security and operations. We don't receive or control those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies

Our own code sets no cookies. The third-party advertising and affiliate services described below may set and read cookies, or use similar technologies such as web beacons and device identifiers, when you visit our pages or click a link. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalized.

To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Travelpayouts

Our pages load a script from Travelpayouts, an affiliate network for travel brands. It may turn links to travel partners (such as airlines, hotels or booking sites) into affiliate links, and Travelpayouts or its partners may use cookies or similar technology to record that a visit or booking came from our site, so we can be paid a commission. This is governed by the Travelpayouts privacy policy.

Affiliate links and partner cookies

Some pages include affiliate links to partners, for example brands on the Impact network (such as Chewy, Trupanion or Lemonade), Amazon.com, Booking.com and Travelpayouts partners. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. When you click an affiliate link, you leave our site and the partner (or its affiliate network) may set cookies or use similar technology to record that the visit came from us. Those cookies are governed by the partner's own privacy policy, not ours. We don't receive personal information about you from these partners, only aggregated sales or commission reports. Affiliate relationships never change the policy information we publish.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. PetPolicyCheck participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program; Amazon may use cookies to record that a visit or purchase came from our links, under Amazon's own privacy notice.

Analytics

We don't run a separate analytics tool (such as Google Analytics) on this site. If we add one, this page will be updated first to name it, what it collects and how to opt out.

External links

We link to airline and government websites. Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@petpolicycheck.com.
