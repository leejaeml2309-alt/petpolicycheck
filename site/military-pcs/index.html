Military · PCS

Military PCS pet move kit

Planning aid for active-duty PCS corridors. Cite official JTR / DFAS pages for reimbursement caps. Not an official DoD publication.

Key corridors

  • Camp Humphreys / Osan (ICN) ↔ USA — APQA export + CDC import; see Korea routes and titer countdown.
  • Ramstein / Spangdahlem (FRA) ↔ USA — EU health certificate timing + CDC Dog Import Form on return.
  • Okinawa / Yokota (NRT/HND/OKA) ↔ USA — Japan MAFF/AQS pathways; often long titer waits.

JTR PCS pet transportation allowance (overview)

DoD Joint Travel Regulations describe pet transportation reimbursement for eligible PCS moves — commonly cited ceilings include up to $550 CONUS and up to $2,000 OCONUS. Confirm current amounts, eligibility, and receipts rules on official defense.gov / dfas.mil publications (verify the date on the JTR table you use).

We do not process claims. Keep itemized airline/kennel receipts for finance.

Patriot Express vs commercial

  • AMC Patriot Express — limited pet slots; book early; heat/breed embargoes can apply. Before a Patriot Express pet space can be booked, provide a signed Pet Risk Acknowledgement Document through your TMC/Transportation Office for upload to GATES. Bookings without it are cancelled. Keep a copy for check-in.
  • Space-A / AMC service dogs — Companion pets are not a leisure Space-A option. Eligible service dogs require separate AMC acceptance and a signed Statement of Assurance no later than 48 hours before departure. The traveller must independently qualify for Space-A; a service dog does not expand route eligibility or guarantee a seat.
  • Patriot Express limits — Patriot Express allows up to two dogs/cats per eligible PCS family, with a maximum combined pet-and-kennel weight of 150 lb. The Seattle gateway limits in-cabin pet bookings to one per ticketed traveller and two per family. Check transit permits too: AMC warns that pets transiting Guam require an entry permit from November 1, 2026.

A universal 48-hour deadline is not the DOT rule: last-minute bookings have a departure-gate submission pathway, and reasonable-effort accommodation requirements apply. AMC service-animal paperwork follows separate rules.

  • Commercial — Delta offers eligible active-duty military/Foreign Service travellers and qualifying dependants a checked-pet option on active transfer orders. It is not a blanket cargo benefit for veterans; flight, route, breed and weather restrictions apply. United’s military pet program is restricted to eligible reassignment travel between Guam and Honolulu. It is not a general military cargo service across all PCS corridors. Confirm acceptance and documentation directly with United. American offers checked-pet transport for eligible active-duty US military and Foreign Service personnel on official orders, subject to restrictions. American PetEmbark cargo is a separate service. Follow current CDC entry rules, not outdated age wording elsewhere on a carrier page. Alaska publishes military baggage perks. Confirm each carrier’s military desk.
