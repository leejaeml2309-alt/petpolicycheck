What to pack for a road trip with your dog: a checklist with gear specs

✓ Product specs checked Oct 22, 2026 against each manufacturer's official page.

Advertise here

A packing list for car trips with a dog. Products are grouped by need; every spec comes from the maker's official page (linked from each product page), and we have not tested them. Rules on restraining pets in cars vary by state and country, so check the rules where you'll drive.

Quick checklist

  • Your dog's food for the whole trip, plus a little extra
  • Water and a travel bowl
  • Leash and a harness with ID tags
  • Poop bags
  • Any medications, and your vet's phone number
  • Vaccination records (some hotels, campgrounds and borders ask for them)
  • A recent photo of your dog, in case you get separated
  • Bedding or a blanket that smells like home
  • Towels and cleanup supplies
