What Petmate says

  • Petmate says the Sky Kennel complies with the standards of the 51st edition (2025) of the IATA Live Animals Regulations, including door, side and back ventilation, and meets most airline cargo specifications.
  • Petmate says it includes 2 "LIVE ANIMAL" stickers, a food and water cup, ID stickers and absorbent material. Only the 21" and 28" sizes include a handle.

Manufacturer claims, not verified by us.
