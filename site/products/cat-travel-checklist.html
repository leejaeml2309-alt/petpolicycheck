Cat travel checklist: carriers, litter, calming and ID

A checklist for travelling with a cat, by car or by plane. Every product spec below comes from the maker's official page, and we have not tested these products.

Quick checklist

  • A secure carrier your cat already knows (leave it out at home for a few days before the trip)
  • Travel litter box, litter, a scoop and bags
  • Food, water and a small bowl
  • Any medications, and your vet's phone number
  • Vaccination and health records (airlines and border authorities may ask for them)
  • Microchip details and a collar with ID
  • A puppy pad or towel to line the carrier
  • Something that smells like home

The carrier

For flights, the carrier usually has to fit under the seat in front of you. Compare carrier sizes with each airline's limit in carrier size by airline and the soft carrier guide. Carriers we list: Sherpa Original Deluxe Pet Carrier, Sleepypod Air, Sleepypod Atom, Sleepypod Mini, SturdiBag Pro 3.0 Travel Pet Carrier.
