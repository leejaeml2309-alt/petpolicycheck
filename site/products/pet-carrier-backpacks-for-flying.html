Pet carrier backpacks for flying: do they fit under the seat?

Backpack carriers are tall, while airline under-seat limits are usually short (8 to 11 in / 20 to 27 cm high). Below, a backpack's published dimensions against each airline limit we've verified, standing upright.

Sherpa Elevation Pet Backpack (Medium)

9 × 13 × 19 in (23 × 33 × 48 cm). Sherpa says the Medium backpack carries pets up to 16 lb (7 kg) that measure 18 in H x 12 in L or less, sitting.
