Free Pet Travel Checklist USA (printable PDF)

A 2-page checklist for traveling in the US with a dog or cat, built from the rules on this site. Every section links back to the page where we cite the official source.

  • Documents: vet health certificate, USDA APHIS endorsement for international trips, rabies certificate, microchip, CDC Dog Import Form
  • Flying: cabin or hold by airline, under-seat carrier sizes
  • Amtrak: weight, carrier, trip length and check-in rules
  • Hotels: what to confirm before you book
  • Departure day: what to pack and when to arrive
