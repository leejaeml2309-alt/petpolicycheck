Step 3

Taxis & airport rides with your pet

From the airport to your hotel: request a pet ride in the Uber or Lyft app (US). Rules below are from their official help pages; there are no affiliate links in this section.

Uber Pet

Pets
One pet per trip: a small animal such as a dog or a cat. Additional pets are at the driver's discretion. No breed or size restrictions.
Fee
Uber Pet costs more than other vehicle options: an Uber Pet surcharge is included in the upfront fare when you select it. A cleaning fee may be charged if your pet leaves waste, excessive hair or damages the vehicle. Uber's driver page says drivers earn an extra Uber Pet fee of $4 in the US ($5 in Canada).
Where
Choose Uber Pet in the vehicle options of the Uber app where it is offered. It is an UberX option served by drivers who opt in (not UberXL or Uber Black); in some areas you can reserve it in advance (reservation fee in the upfront fare).
Tips
Restrain your pet with a leash, harness or crate/carrier, bring a blanket or towel for the seat, and ask the driver where the pet should sit.

Official source: Uber Help: Uber Pet (riders) ↗ · Uber Help: Uber Pet (drivers) ↗ · Uber Help: Tolls, surcharges and fees ↗

Lyft Pet

Pets
One well-behaved animal, plus up to four people. As a courtesy, the pet should ride in a carrier, on a blanket or at your feet.
Fee
An additional fee that goes directly to the driver. Lyft's driver page says drivers earn a $4 bonus for every Pet ride.
Where
U.S. only. Select the Pet ride option in the Lyft app.

Official source: Lyft Help: Pet rides for riders ↗ · Lyft Help: Pet rides for drivers ↗

Robotaxis: service animals only

  • Waymo: No pets. Service animals small enough to fit ride with riders with disabilities; emotional support and therapy animals are not allowed. Waymo Help: Service animals ↗
  • Zoox: No pets or emotional support animals. Trained service dogs are welcome (kept on the floor during the ride). Zoox Support FAQ ↗
  • Tesla Robotaxi: No pets. Disabled riders may bring service animals. Service in limited areas of Miami, Orlando and Tampa (FL) and Austin, Dallas and Houston (TX). Tesla Support: Robotaxi ↗

Service animals ride on Uber and Lyft at no extra charge even without a Pet ride (Uber and Lyft help pages). Checked on the official Uber, Lyft, Waymo, Zoox and Tesla pages on Oct 24, 2026; fees and availability change, so check the app before you ride.

Before you fly

Vet clinics & health certificates

Flying abroad, or to some states, usually means a health certificate, up-to-date rabies vaccination and sometimes a microchip, tests or a permit, all within strict time windows. In the US, a USDA-accredited veterinarian issues the certificate and USDA APHIS endorses it for international travel. Book that vet visit early.

They will appear here and on Partner vets by country and city as soon as they confirm. Until then, use the official tools below to find a vet who can sign your pet’s health certificate.

Find an accredited vet: official tools

  • USDA APHIS: Find an accredited veterinarian (search by state and county) ↗
    The official NVAP search tool. Choose your state and county and Category I (dogs and cats). Not every accredited vet chooses to be listed, so you can also ask your own vet whether someone in the practice is USDA-accredited.
  • USDA APHIS: How do I find a USDA-accredited veterinarian? ↗
    Most countries require the health certificate to be issued by a USDA-accredited vet and then endorsed by APHIS. Accreditation is state-specific: the vet must be accredited in the state where you see them.
  • USDA APHIS: Take a pet from the US to another country ↗
    Step-by-step export process and each destination country's requirements. Contact an accredited vet as soon as you decide to travel.
  • USDA APHIS: Pet travel (all topics) ↗
    Domestic and international pet travel, including which animals count as pets and the CDC rules for dogs.

Official USDA pages checked Oct 24, 2026. We do not book vet appointments and are not paid for referrals.

Paperwork by destination

  • Pet travel rules for the USA: federal rules and each state’s health certificate and rabies rules.
  • USA to Japan: the Japan health certificate (Form AC) completed by a USDA-accredited vet, microchip, rabies titer test and the 180-day wait.
  • USA to South Korea: ISO microchip, rabies vaccination, a rabies antibody (FAVN) test and a health certificate endorsed by USDA APHIS within 30 days of export.
