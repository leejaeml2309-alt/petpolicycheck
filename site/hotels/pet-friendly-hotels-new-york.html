Pet-friendly hotels in New York City: chain and luxury hotel pet fees, weight limits and local tips

New York City is the second most-searched US city for pet-friendly hotels. Space is tight and rules vary a lot by hotel, and some budget chains' NYC locations take no pets at all. Below: each chain's brand-wide rules from its official site, plus sourced NYC tips for JFK and the city's dog rules.

Policies change: confirm with the hotel. Pet fees, weight limits and the number of pets are set by each brand and often by each hotel, and can change without notice. We read every chain’s official page on Oct 24, 2026. Call or message the hotel to confirm before you book, and declare your pet at check-in.

Quick answer

  • No pet fee: Kimpton charges no pet fee and sets no size, weight or number limit; Motel 6 lets up to 2 dogs or cats stay free (150 lb combined). Source
  • Budget chains: Red Roof: first pet free, second $15 per night, 80 lb per pet. La Quinta: optional fee up to $25 per night, max $75 per stay, 2 pets. Source
  • Higher fees: Drury charges a daily pet fee of at least $50 (varies by hotel), 2 pets, 80 lb combined. Source
  • Hilton, Aloft, Four Seasons, Loews: Their official pages give no single brand-wide pet fee or weight limit (Loews allows 2 pets): each hotel sets its own rules, so check the hotel page. Source
  • NYC dog rules: Dogs must be on a leash of 6 ft or less in city parks except in dog runs and off-leash areas (park opening to 9 a.m. and 9 p.m. to closing). NYC requires owners to carry proof of a current dog license and rabies vaccination in public. Source

JFK Airport pet relief areas

JFK says Service Animal Relief Areas are available at all terminals for service animals and pets, with indoor and outdoor spaces, artificial grass, sinks and waste stations. Use JFK's interactive map to find the one nearest your gate. Official source

Dogs in NYC parks

NYC Parks rules for dogs, from the official NYC Parks page: Official source

  • Leash of no more than 6 feet at all times, except in dog runs (fenced, off-leash during park hours) and designated off-leash areas from park opening to 9 a.m. and from 9 p.m. to closing.
  • Carry proof of a current dog license and rabies vaccination while in public (NYC Health Code).
  • No dogs in playgrounds, fountains, ball fields, courts, pools or on NYC beaches in summer; dogs may go on the sand from October 2 to the Friday before Memorial Day.
  • Manhattan dog runs include Madison Square Park, Washington Square Park, Tribeca (Warren St), Riverside Park (W 72nd, 87th and 105th St), Theodore Roosevelt Park (CPW at W 81st St) and Hudson River Park (Pier 40 and Pier 84).
  • Central Park has no fenced dog run but 23 dog-friendly areas (see the Central Park Conservancy's guide linked from NYC Parks).

Before you book

  • Filter for pet-friendly, then open the hotel’s own policy: fee per night or per stay, weight limit, number of pets, and whether cats are accepted.
  • Ask whether your pet may stay alone in the room: Motel 6 and Drury say pets should not be left alone.
  • Service animals are not pets. Ask the hotel how it handles service animals and emotional support animals rather than assuming a pet fee or limit applies.
