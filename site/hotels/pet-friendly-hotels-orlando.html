Pet-friendly hotels in Orlando: chain and luxury hotel pet fees, weight limits and local tips

Orlando is the most-searched US city for pet-friendly hotels. Most big chains take pets here, but fees, weight limits and the number of pets differ by brand and by hotel. Below: the brand-wide rules from each chain's official site, plus sourced Orlando tips for the airport and dog parks.

Policies change: confirm with the hotel. Pet fees, weight limits and the number of pets are set by each brand and often by each hotel, and can change without notice. We read every chain’s official page on Oct 24, 2026. Call or message the hotel to confirm before you book, and declare your pet at check-in.

Quick answer

  • No pet fee: Kimpton charges no pet fee and sets no size, weight or number limit; Motel 6 lets up to 2 dogs or cats stay free (150 lb combined). Source
  • Budget chains: Red Roof: first pet free, second $15 per night, 80 lb per pet. La Quinta: optional fee up to $25 per night, max $75 per stay, 2 pets. Source
  • Higher fees: Drury charges a daily pet fee of at least $50 (varies by hotel), 2 pets, 80 lb combined. Source
  • Hilton, Aloft, Four Seasons, Loews: Their official pages give no single brand-wide pet fee or weight limit (Loews allows 2 pets): each hotel sets its own rules, so check the hotel page. Source
  • Orlando International Airport (MCO): Relief areas before security at Terminals A and B (Level 2) and after security near each gate group and in Terminal C; dogs inside the terminal must be service dogs or in an airline-approved carrier. Source

Orlando International Airport (MCO) pet relief areas

Dogs are not allowed inside MCO unless they are working dogs or in a carrier approved for the flight. Service animal relief areas, open to pets: Official source

  • Before security: Terminal A, Level 2, east end; Terminal B, Level 2, west end; and the east end of the North Cell Phone Lot.
  • After security, Terminals A and B: Gates 1-29 (on the wing for Gates 20-29), Gates 30-59 (wing for Gates 40-49), Gates 70-99 (wing for Gates 90-99), Gates 100-129 (across from Gate 121).
  • After security, Terminal C: Level 2 near Gates C238, C241 and C242, and Level 6 just before international bag claim.
  • Pets shipped as cargo are picked up at the airline's cargo facility, not in the passenger terminal.

Off-leash dog parks in Orange County

Orange County Parks runs fenced off-leash dog parks across the Orlando area. Dogs must wear a current license and tags, be leashed going in and out, and owners must stay inside within sight and voice range. West Orange and Downey also have a small-dog area and water fountains. Official source

Chain notes for Orlando

Official source

  • Loews runs the hotels at Universal Orlando Resort (Portofino Bay, Hard Rock Hotel, Royal Pacific, Sapphire Falls, Cabana Bay and others); they appear on the Loews Loves Pets page. Loews allows up to 2 pets per room with up-to-date vaccinations, and property-specific rules and cleaning fees may apply, so confirm with your Universal hotel.

Before you book

  • Filter for pet-friendly, then open the hotel’s own policy: fee per night or per stay, weight limit, number of pets, and whether cats are accepted.
  • Ask whether your pet may stay alone in the room: Motel 6 and Drury say pets should not be left alone.
  • Service animals are not pets. Ask the hotel how it handles service animals and emotional support animals rather than assuming a pet fee or limit applies.
