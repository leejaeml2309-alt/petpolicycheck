Pet-friendly hotels in Detroit: chain and hotel pet fees, weight limits and pet numbers

Most big chains take pets in Detroit, but fees, weight limits and the number of pets differ by brand and by hotel. Below: brand-wide rules from each chain’s official site, plus 5 Detroit hotels whose own official pages publish a pet policy.

Policies change: confirm with the hotel. Pet fees, weight limits and the number of pets are set by each brand and often by each hotel, and can change without notice. We read each hotel’s official page on Nov 2, 2026. Call or message the hotel to confirm before you book, and declare your pet at check-in.

Quick answer

  • No pet fee in Detroit: none of the 5 hotels below (from each hotel’s official page). See the hotels
  • Chains with no fee: Kimpton promises no pet fee and no size, weight or number limit brand-wide; Motel 6 lets up to 2 dogs or cats stay free (150 lb combined). Source
  • Budget chains: Red Roof: first pet free, second $15 per night, 80 lb per pet. La Quinta: optional fee up to $25 per night, max $75 per stay, 2 pets. Source
  • Hotel by hotel: Hilton, Marriott, Loews and luxury hotels set rules per property, so read the hotel’s own page: fees in Detroit range from $0 to $500 per stay below.

Before you book

  • Filter for pet-friendly, then open the hotel’s own policy: fee per night or per stay, weight limit, number of pets, and whether cats are accepted.
  • Ask whether your pet may stay alone in the room: several hotels above say pets must not be left unattended.
  • Service animals are not pets. Ask the hotel how it handles service animals and emotional support animals rather than assuming a pet fee or limit applies.
