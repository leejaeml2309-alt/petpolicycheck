Pet-friendly hotels in Miami: chain pet fees, weight limits and local tips

Miami rounds out our top three US cities for pet-friendly hotel searches. Beach and luxury hotels often have stricter pet rules than airport and highway chains, so compare the brand rules below and confirm with your hotel. Below: each chain's brand-wide rules from its official site, plus sourced Miami tips for MIA and county dog parks.

Policies change: confirm with the hotel. Pet fees, weight limits and the number of pets are set by each brand and often by each hotel, and can change without notice. We read every chain’s official page on Oct 24, 2026. Call or message the hotel to confirm before you book, and declare your pet at check-in.

Quick answer

  • No pet fee: Kimpton charges no pet fee and sets no size, weight or number limit; Motel 6 lets up to 2 dogs or cats stay free (150 lb combined). Source
  • Budget chains: Red Roof: first pet free, second $15 per night, 80 lb per pet. La Quinta: optional fee up to $25 per night, max $75 per stay, 2 pets. Source
  • Higher fees: Drury charges a daily pet fee of at least $50 (varies by hotel), 2 pets, 80 lb combined. Source
  • Hilton, Aloft, Four Seasons, Loews: Their official pages give no single brand-wide pet fee or weight limit (Loews allows 2 pets): each hotel sets its own rules, so check the hotel page. Source
  • Miami International Airport (MIA): 3 outdoor relief areas on Level 1 Baggage Claim/Arrivals (near D, F and J; the J area is temporarily closed for construction) and 4 indoor ones after security (D34, F7, G11, H-J connector). Source

Miami International Airport (MIA) pet relief areas

MIA lists these service animal and pet relief areas: Official source

  • Outdoor, near parking (Level 1 Baggage Claim/Arrivals): west of the North (Dolphin) garage near Terminal D, across from Door 5; west of the South Short Term lot near Terminal F, across from Door 15; south of the Flamingo garage near Terminal J, across from Door 28 (temporarily closed for construction).
  • Indoor, after security: North Terminal (D) near Gate D34; Concourse F near Gate F7; Concourse G near Gate G11; Concourse H-J connector, across from the InMotion store.

Miami-Dade County dog parks

Miami-Dade Parks has three kinds of dog parks. Posted rules: vaccinations up to date, a current rabies tag on the collar, leash until inside the fence, no more than 3 dogs per person, no puppies under 4 months. The county page also still lists 'Pitbulls are not allowed', citing the county ordinance. A 2023 Florida law (HB 941) ended local breed-specific bans, so this line may be outdated: ask the park before you go. Official source

  • Free-roaming dog parks: Haulover Park (10800 Collins Ave.), Tropical Park (7900 SW 40 St.), Amelia Earhart Park (401 E 65 St., Hialeah), East Greynolds Park (16700 Biscayne Blvd., North Miami Beach), West Kendall District Park (11255 SW 157 Ave).
  • Dog run park (no water or bag dispensers): Lago Mar Park (8165 SW 162 Ave.).
  • Haulover Park also has a dog beach with its own rules (linked from the county page).

Chain notes for Miami

Official source

  • Loews Miami Beach Hotel (South Beach) and Loews Coral Gables Hotel appear on the Loews Loves Pets page: up to 2 pets, vaccinations required, property rules and cleaning fees may apply.
  • Four Seasons Hotel Miami lists pet-friendly rooms but no weight or fee on its amenities page: ask the hotel. Source

Before you book

  • Filter for pet-friendly, then open the hotel’s own policy: fee per night or per stay, weight limit, number of pets, and whether cats are accepted.
  • Ask whether your pet may stay alone in the room: Motel 6 and Drury say pets should not be left alone.
  • Service animals are not pets. Ask the hotel how it handles service animals and emotional support animals rather than assuming a pet fee or limit applies.
