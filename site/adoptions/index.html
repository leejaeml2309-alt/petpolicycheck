Adoptions

Why this section matters to us

PetPolicyCheck was born from a love of animals, and adoption is where that love began. Over the years, I have supported animal rescue in France and in Korea, through donations and as a volunteer driver, helping rescued dogs reach their new families, including Jolie, a Korean dog who flew to her adoptive family in the United States. At home, all three of our companions were abandoned animals before they found us. This section is our way of giving back: clear, official information so that more animals find a safe, lasting home.

— Marc, founder of PetPolicyCheck
