Pet policy updates

Changes we've found on airline pet policy pages, newest first. Changes our nightly checker detects are logged here once reviewed.

  • Aug 3, 2026 Delta Air Lines: Dogs entering Mexico need a New World Screwworm Inspection Certificate issued no more than 5 days before arrival, verified at check-in. source
  • Jul 25, 2026 Air Canada: Travellers reported a temporary in-cabin pet embargo for July 25 - August 1, 2026, later cancelled. Not shown on Air Canada's official page as of 2026-10-22: unconfirmed by the airline. source unofficial source
  • Jun 1, 2025 Air Canada: All pets travelling in the cabin must be in soft-sided carriers. source
  • Apr 8, 2025 Delta Air Lines: In-cabin pet fee for U.S./Canada/PR/USVI rose from $95 to $150 each way for tickets issued on/after April 8, 2025. source
  • Oct 1, 2024 Lufthansa: Dogs to Newark (EWR) possible again if not in a high-risk rabies area in the prior 6 months. source
  • Aug 1, 2024 Lufthansa: After CDC dog-entry rule changes, Lufthansa accepts new dog bookings to the USA only to ATL, LAX, MIA, JFK, PHL and Washington DC. source
  • not yet verified United Airlines: United no longer accepts checked pets through PetSafe; cargo only for certain active-duty military / State Department staff. (No effective date given on the page.) source
