What this forum is for

A place for pet owners to share first-hand experiences of travelling with a dog or cat, so others can prepare before their own trip. Every post and comment is checked by a moderator before it appears. Experiences are organised by:

  • Transport: flights in the cabin or the hold, trains, buses, subways, taxis, cars and ferries.
  • Weather and season: hot or cold days, rain or snow, and how the temperature affected the trip.
  • Carrier: soft bag, hard crate, stroller or harness only, including what fitted under the seat and what staff checked.
  • Pet, breed and size: dog or cat, the breed the owner shared, and a weight band.

Start here

  • Pet Travel Stories: summaries of public discussions about flying and moving abroad with dogs and cats, with short quotes and links to the original threads.
  • Community rules: what you can post and how moderation works.
  • Airline pet policies: official cabin and hold rules for each airline we cover, checked against the airline's own page and dated.
