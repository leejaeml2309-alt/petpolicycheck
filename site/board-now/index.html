Fast track

Board now

Your flight is booked. Here is the shortest safe path to the gate with your dog or cat.

Three questions decide everything: does your carrier fit under the seat, is your pet within the airline’s rules, and what does your destination require? Answer them in that order.

I already have a carrierI need a carrierUnder-seat sizesRules by destination

Path 1

I already have a carrier

  1. Measure the outside. Length, width and height of the carrier itself, the way airlines publish their limits.
  2. Compare with your airline. Use the under-seat table below. In our comparisons length and width may swap (you can turn the carrier); height may not.
  3. Check the carrier type and weight. Air Canada, Air France and Alaska & Hawaiian accept soft-sided carriers only; Air France and Lufthansa cap pet + carrier at 8 kg.
  4. Reserve the pet space with the airline before the trip, then check your destination’s paperwork below.

Want the exact overage, dimension by dimension? Try the carrier fit check.

Path 2

I need a carrier

One real example per pet size. Size bands are each maker’s published pet-weight limit, not breed standards. “Fits” compares the maker’s published dimensions with the general limits we checked on 28 November 2026; the gate agent always has the final say.
