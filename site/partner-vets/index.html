Partner vets: travel-ready clinics

Flying with a dog or cat usually means a health certificate, up-to-date vaccinations (often a rabies titer test) and country-specific paperwork, all within strict time windows. Our partner veterinary clinics help pet owners prepare these documents ahead of the trip, so nothing is missing at check-in or at the border.

Partner clinics are independent practices. PetPolicyCheck does not provide veterinary advice and is not paid for referrals; always confirm requirements with the clinic and the destination country's official authority.
