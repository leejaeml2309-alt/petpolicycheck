Travelling with a dog or cat between the USA and Mexico

Since 3 August 2026, US-origin dogs need a New World screwworm (NWS) inspection certificate signed by a USDA-accredited vet within 5 days of entry into Mexico (no APHIS endorsement). Cats still need no health certificate. Returning dogs also need a SENASICA screwworm-freedom certificate plus the CDC Dog Import Form.

Quick answer

  • Official rule Dogs US → Mexico (since 3 Aug 2026): NWS Inspection Certificate for Pet Dogs Moving from the US to Mexico, signed by a USDA-accredited veterinarian within 5 days before entry. No APHIS endorsement. Missing or improper NWS paperwork can mean denied entry or holding at the border. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Cats US → Mexico: no health certificate and no NWS document since 16 Dec 2019. Do not add cats to the dog NWS form — that invalidates it. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule At Mexico OISA (SENASICA): pet must show no disease signs, no ectoparasites, no fresh/healing wounds. Dirty bedding, toys and extra food are removed/destroyed; only that day’s food ration is allowed. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Return dogs Mexico → US: Mexico has been screwworm-affected since 22 Nov 2024. Every dog needs a screwworm-freedom certificate from a SENASICA-approved vet (inspection ≤5 days before travel) PLUS CDC Dog Import Form, age ≥6 months, microchip, healthy appearance. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026
  • Official rule Mexico is not on the CDC high-risk dog-rabies list (low-risk for CDC rabies rules). Screwworm rules are separate USDA/SENASICA requirements. Source Checked Nov 9, 2026

Rules change. Confirm on the official pages linked in each row before you book. Last checked Nov 9, 2026.

Checklist — USA → Mexico

  • Dogs: NWS inspection certificate ≤5 days (USDA-accredited vet, no APHIS endorsement)
  • Cats: no health certificate for Mexico entry
  • Clean carrier; only day’s food ration
  • Airline pet booking

FAQ

Do dogs need APHIS endorsement to enter Mexico?

No for the NWS dog inspection certificate effective 3 Aug 2026 — a USDA-accredited vet signs it directly. Checked Nov 9, 2026 on APHIS Mexico page.

Can I put my cat on the dog NWS form?

No. APHIS warns that adding cats invalidates the dog form and can lead to denial or holding. Checked Nov 9, 2026.

Is Mexico high-risk for CDC dog rabies?

No — Mexico is not on the CDC high-risk list. Screwworm certification is a separate USDA requirement. Checked Nov 9, 2026.
