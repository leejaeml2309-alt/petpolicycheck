Flying a pet Mexico to USA on Delta Air Lines

Verified snapshot for corridor planning. Cabin/cargo numbers from our airline dataset (checked 2026-10-22). Destination summary cites CDC.

Résumé d'entrée

CDC Dog Import Form; Mexico risk classification per CDC; screwworm inspection certificate may apply for return corridors.

Delta Air Lines

  • Frais cabine publiés: U.S./Canada/Puerto Rico/U.S. Virgin Islands: $150 USD/CAD each way (tickets issued on/after Apr 8, 2025; $95 before) · International: $200 USD/CAD/EUR each way · Brazil: $200 USD each way (tickets issued on/after Apr 8, 2025; $75 before)
  • Cage cabine / sous le siège: Depends on aircraft under-seat space; Delta recommends a soft-sided kennel of max 18 x 11 x 11 in, which fits most aircraft
  • Poids: See official airline page (weight not in verified snapshot)
  • Soute / cargo: No checked pets for the general public. Delta Cargo ships pets only for active U.S. Military or U.S. State Department Foreign Service personnel with permanent change of station orders (until further notice).
  • Note animal d'assistance: Trained service animals follow separate guidelines (see Delta's service animal page).
  • Sources: Official airline page ↗ · verified 2026-10-22
