Travelling with a dog or cat between the USA and Australia

The United States (excluding Guam and Hawaii) is a DAFF Group 3 approved country for cat and dog imports. Plan for an import permit through BICON, an ISO-compatible microchip, a rabies neutralizing antibody titre test (RNATT) and a mandatory 180-day wait after the sample arrives at the laboratory, USDA APHIS-endorsed documents, parasite treatments, and post-entry quarantine at Mickleham after a direct arrival at Melbourne. Returning dogs follow CDC rabies-free or low-risk rules if they have not been in a high-risk country.

Quick answer

  • Official rule The United States of America (excluding Guam and Hawaii) is listed as a Group 3 approved country on DAFF’s Group 3 step-by-step guides for dogs and cats. Check those lists and BICON if you are leaving from Hawaii, Guam or another territory. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · DAFF cats Checked Nov 14, 2026
  • Official rule Apply for a biosecurity import permit in BICON. Group 3 applications must include the RNATT laboratory report and the corresponding RNATT declaration endorsed by the official government veterinarian. A Group 3 permit is valid until the RNATT expires — 12 months from the date the blood sample is taken. DAFF says to allow at least 6 months to complete the import steps, and that most permits are issued in 20–40 business days (it can take up to 123). Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · BICON Checked Nov 14, 2026
  • Official rule Implant a working ISO-compatible microchip (generally 10 or 15 digits; numbers starting with 999 are not accepted) before you start the process. A government-approved veterinarian must scan it at every visit and before RNATT blood collection, and the number must match every document. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source Checked Nov 14, 2026
  • Official rule After rabies vaccination, a government-approved veterinarian in an approved country draws the RNATT sample. The animal cannot be exported to Australia for at least 180 days after the sample arrives at the laboratory — DAFF says there are no exceptions, and this is a residency period, not quarantine. The sample must be taken between 12 months and 180 days before the date of export. USDA APHIS describes the same 180-day wait and the endorsed RNATT declaration via VEHCS. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · USDA APHIS Checked Nov 14, 2026
  • Official rule Internal parasite treatments (nematodes and cestodes) twice within 45 days before export, at least 14 days apart, with the last treatment within 5 days of export. External treatments must be topical and kill ticks and fleas on contact (oral products and tick collars are not accepted). A government-approved veterinarian must give these treatments. A final clinical examination is required within 5 days before export. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source Checked Nov 14, 2026
  • Official rule USDA APHIS: a USDA-accredited veterinarian prepares the pet; identity declarations, the RNATT declaration and the export health certificate are endorsed through VEHCS. Carry the printed, endorsed documents (including laboratory reports) with the animal. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026
  • Official rule Cats and dogs that must complete post-entry quarantine travel directly to Melbourne International Airport as manifest air cargo, then go to the Mickleham PEQ facility — the only government cat-and-dog post-entry quarantine facility. Home quarantine is not offered. The minimum stay is 10 or 30 days (up to 180 days if there is a biosecurity issue). A 10-day stay for a Group 3 animal requires an official identity declaration (or an Australian export health certificate for Australian-origin pets) with the permit application. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · DAFF FAQ Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Permit / 10-day Checked Nov 14, 2026
  • Official rule Return to the US: Australia is not on CDC’s high-risk dog-rabies list, so CDC treats it as dog-rabies-free or low-risk if the dog has been only in such countries for the 6 months before entry. Dogs need a CDC Dog Import Form receipt (free), a detectable microchip, to be at least 6 months old and to appear healthy, and may arrive at any airport, seaport or land border. The CDC Dog Import Form does not apply to cats; confirm cat entry on official APHIS and CBP pages. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · CDC high-risk list Checked Nov 14, 2026 · CDC Dog Import Form Checked Nov 14, 2026 · USDA APHIS pet travel Checked Nov 14, 2026

Rules change. Confirm every step on the official DAFF, BICON, APHIS and CDC pages linked here before you book or draw blood. This page is a summary, not legal or veterinary advice, last checked Nov 14, 2026.

Checklist — USA → Australia

  • Confirm the US state or territory of residence is a DAFF approved (Group 3) origin — the 48 contiguous states plus listed US territories on the Group 3 guides; Hawaii and Guam are excluded from that US listing
  • Working ISO-compatible microchip in place and scanned at every official visit
  • Rabies vaccination, then RNATT blood draw in an approved country; 180-day wait after the sample arrives at the lab
  • USDA-endorsed RNATT declaration and, if you want a 10-day PEQ stay, official identity declaration
  • BICON import permit; then book Mickleham only after the permit is issued
  • Internal and external parasite treatments on DAFF’s timetable, given by a government-approved veterinarian
  • Final exam within 5 days of export and APHIS-endorsed export health certificate (printed) plus laboratory reports travelling with the animal
  • Direct manifest air-cargo arrival at Melbourne (MEL); collection to Mickleham

Melbourne arrival and Mickleham

DAFF requires cats and dogs being imported to travel directly to Melbourne International Airport as manifest air cargo (DAFF and Qantas Freight both state that arrivals from New Zealand or Norfolk Island are treated differently — confirm that exception on the official pages if it applies). Officers collect the animal at Melbourne and take it to the Post Entry Quarantine facility at 135 Donnybrook Road, Mickleham, Victoria 3064. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Qantas Freight Checked Nov 14, 2026

DAFF publishes accepted flight-arrival windows for Mickleham bookings (for example, Monday, Tuesday, Saturday and Sunday between 5:00 and 14:30, and Wednesday–Friday between 5:00 and 21:00; flights scheduled to land between 21:00 and 05:00 AEST are not accepted). Out-of-hours collection fees can apply. An import permit does not guarantee a reservation date. Book through the Post Entry Biosecurity System after the permit is issued, and pay the part of the fee due at booking. Release is from the Mickleham facility, not the airport terminal. Confirm current windows, booking and collection rules on the facility page. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source Checked Nov 14, 2026

Airlines between the US and Australia

Australia’s official import path is manifest air cargo into Melbourne, not an in-cabin pet on a passenger ticket. We only list airlines whose official pet or cargo-pet pages we could verify for this corridor.

  • Qantas Freight — kept. Official international pet pages state that pets arriving into Australia must travel on a direct flight into Melbourne (unless travelling from New Zealand or Norfolk Island), and that international movements are booked through a pet travel specialist or Qantas Freight. The US Freight page sets out booking documents (import permit number, PEQ prepayment, kennel dimensions), a Los Angeles Freight drop-off, temperature limits for Australia–US uplifts, and breed and age conditions. Confirm current routes, seasonal port embargoes and prices with Qantas Freight. Official pages: International pet travel · Qantas Freight US pets. Route pages: USA → Australia on Qantas · Australia → USA on Qantas.
  • United — dropped. United’s official travelling-with-pets page says pets cannot fly to, from or through Australia, and that the PetSafe checked-pet program has ended except for certain military or State Department employees. United official page.
  • Delta — dropped. Delta’s official in-cabin pet page lists Australia among destinations where pets are not permitted in the cabin. Delta Cargo’s official pets page states a temporary global embargo on household pet shipments (limited exception for some military or Foreign Service officers on permanent-change-of-station orders). Delta pet travel · Delta Cargo pets.
  • American Airlines / PetEmbark — dropped. We could not verify on American’s official PetEmbark pages that they accept household pets as manifest cargo into Melbourne, which DAFF requires for this import path. We do not list them for this corridor.
  • Air New Zealand Cargo — dropped. Air New Zealand Cargo’s official international pets page describes cargo-pet carriage via approved transporters but is written around New Zealand export and does not, on that page, verify a USA–Australia pet routing into Melbourne. We do not list them until that is clear on an official page.

Timeline

  1. Well before six months: Confirm eligibility on the Group 3 guides and in BICON. Implant and test the microchip. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · BICON Checked Nov 14, 2026
  2. RNATT: After rabies vaccination, have a government-approved veterinarian draw the sample. The 180-day residency wait starts when the sample arrives at the laboratory. The sample must also fall between 12 months and 180 days before export. Get the RNATT declaration endorsed through USDA APHIS / VEHCS. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · APHIS Checked Nov 14, 2026
  3. Permit: Apply in BICON with the required Group 3 documents. DAFF asks you to allow at least 6 months for the whole process; permit assessment is often 20–40 business days. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026
  4. Mickleham booking: Only after a permit is issued. Pay the reservation amount due at booking. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026
  5. Final weeks: External parasite cover from at least 21 days before export (count the first treatment day as day 0, and repeat to the manufacturer’s shortest interval). Two internal treatments within 45 days, 14 days apart, last one within 5 days of export. Final exam and APHIS-endorsed health certificate within 5 days of export. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · APHIS Checked Nov 14, 2026
  6. Travel day: Manifest cargo on a direct Melbourne arrival in an accepted time window. Mickleham collects from the airport. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026

Return to the USA

Checklist — Australia → USA (dogs)

  • Confirm Australia remains off CDC’s high-risk dog-rabies list, and that the dog has not been in a high-risk country in the past 6 months
  • Complete a CDC Dog Import Form for each dog and carry the receipt (print or phone)
  • Dog at least 6 months old, microchip readable with a universal scanner, appears healthy on arrival
  • Any airline or cargo-pet documents Qantas Freight or your specialist requires, plus export paperwork Australia may require — confirm those on official DAFF export and airline pages; we have not added unofficial export steps here

Cats are not covered by the CDC Dog Import Form. Use official USDA APHIS and CBP pet-travel pages for cats entering the United States, and confirm with the airline. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · CDC dogs Checked Nov 14, 2026

FAQ

Can my pet arrive in Sydney, Brisbane or Los Angeles–Sydney on a passenger ticket and then go to quarantine?

DAFF’s published import conditions for cats and dogs require travel directly to Melbourne International Airport as manifest air cargo, then transport to Mickleham. Qantas Freight states the same Melbourne-direct rule (with a published exception for New Zealand and Norfolk Island). Confirm any exception only on those official pages. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Qantas Freight Checked Nov 14, 2026

Is the 180-day RNATT wait quarantine?

No. DAFF states it is a residency period in an approved country after the RNATT sample arrives at the laboratory, and that there are no exceptions. The animal still completes post-entry quarantine after arrival in Australia. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026

Why might quarantine be 10 days versus 30 days?

DAFF says all cats and dogs stay at Mickleham for at least 10–30 days (and up to 180 if needed). For a Group 3 animal to qualify for a minimum 10-day period, the permit application must include an official identity declaration endorsed by the official government veterinarian (or an Australian export health certificate for Australian-origin pets), and the microchip on that document must identify the animal. Follow the issued permit. Source Checked Nov 14, 2026 · Source Checked Nov 14, 2026
