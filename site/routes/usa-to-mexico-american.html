Flying a pet USA to Mexico on American Airlines

Verified snapshot for corridor planning. Cabin/cargo numbers from our airline dataset (checked 2026-10-22). Destination summary cites SENASICA.

Destination entry summary

Mexico SENASICA/OISA: physical inspection; dogs from USA may need ≤5-day screwworm (NWS) vet attestation (2026 guidance); vaccination booklet.

American Airlines

  • Published cabin fee: Carry-on: U.S., Canada, Mexico, Central America, Caribbean: $150 per kennel each way · Checked (active-duty military / State Dept only): $200 per kennel; $150 to/from Brazil · Cargo (PetEmbark): Varies
  • Under-seat / cabin carrier: Soft-sided (recommended): 18 x 11 x 11 in (46 x 28 x 28 cm); hard-sided mainline: 19 x 13 x 9 in; hard-sided American Eagle: 16 x 12 x 8 in
  • Weight notes: 20 lb (9.07 kg) pet + carrier on A321T premium cabins; checked kennel max 100 lb (45 kg) combined. No general in-cabin limit stated.
  • Hold / cargo: Checked pets only for active-duty U.S. Military and State Department Foreign Service on official orders. Others: American Airlines Cargo (PetEmbark).
  • Service animal note: Fully-trained service dogs fly in the cabin at no charge if they meet requirements.
  • Sources: Official airline page ↗ · verified 2026-10-22
