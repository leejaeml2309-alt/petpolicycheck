Corrections to airline data

Found a pet policy detail that's out of date or wrong? Please email us with the airline, what's wrong, and a link to the airline's official page showing the current rule. We check every correction against the official source before updating, and the page's "last verified" date changes when we do.

Partnerships & sponsorship

Pet travel brands, insurers and travel companies interested in partnerships or sponsorship are welcome to get in touch. Sponsorship never changes the airline policy data we publish (see our disclosure).

We can't book flights or answer questions about a specific reservation: please contact your airline for that.
