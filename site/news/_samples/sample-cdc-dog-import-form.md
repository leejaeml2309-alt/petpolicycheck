This is a **sample** news item for the PetPolicyCheck /news/ preview. It is not a published update.

On its official [CDC Dog Import Form and Instructions](https://www.cdc.gov/importation/dogs/dog-import-form-instructions.html) page (page date 28 April 2026), CDC states:

- A CDC Dog Import Form is required for each dog you are bringing into the U.S.
- On 5 February 2026, the CDC Dog Import Form web system was updated. The update provides a new look and formatting for the receipt — there are no changes to importation requirements.
- If you have already submitted a form using the previous version, your receipt will still be accepted for entry as long as it contains accurate information and is not expired.

CDC also states that after you submit the form and confirm your email address, you will receive a receipt, which you can print or show on your phone. If the dog is traveling by air, you must show the receipt to the airline before boarding, and you must be able to show it to U.S. Customs and Border Protection when the dog arrives in the United States.

For dogs that have been only in dog rabies-free or low-risk countries in the 6 months before U.S. entry, CDC says this is the only form that is needed. U.S.-vaccinated dogs and foreign-vaccinated dogs that have been in a high-risk country in the 6 months before U.S. entry have additional requirements, set out on the same official CDC page.

Facts on this sample were checked against the official CDC page on 20 November 2026. Rules can change; confirm on the official source before you travel.
