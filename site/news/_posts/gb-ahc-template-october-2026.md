People who need an Animal Health Certificate issued in Great Britain for a dog, cat or ferret returning from the EU or another part 1 listed country, and official veterinarians who issue those certificates.

A new Animal Health Certificate form, introduced when European Union pet travel rules changed on 22 April 2026, became the required version for official veterinarians in Great Britain on 1 October 2026. The previous form could still be issued through 30 September 2026, under a transition agreed by the EU. From 1 October 2026, veterinarians must issue the new form. Certificates issued in Great Britain are accepted for dogs, cats and ferrets returning from the EU or other part 1 listed countries. Each certificate remains valid for six months from the issue date, or until the animal’s rabies vaccination expires, whichever is earlier. Official guidance notes that both versions may still be in circulation for a time.

The official GOV.UK page was updated on 1 October 2026. The page does not state a separate announcement date for the 1 October deadline.
