Veteran snowbirds & VA / military lodging

  • Canada → US (snowbird): CDC Dog Import Form receipt — often usable for months for dogs from dog-rabies-free/low-risk countries (confirm CDC). Keep rabies docs current.
  • Mexico: SENASICA/OISA entry; returning to the US may need a ≤5-day screwworm inspection certificate for dogs on affected corridors.
  • Fisher House: accredited service dogs welcome; companion pets generally not.
  • Navy Lodge / IHG Army Hotels: service dogs free; companion pets only in designated pet-friendly rooms (fee/weight rules vary).
