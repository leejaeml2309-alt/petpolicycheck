About & disclaimer

Why this site exists

Airline pet policies change often, sometimes without notice, and many pages online are outdated or contradict each other. We check each airline's official pet policy page, record the date we checked, and link to it. If a detail isn't on the official page, or we couldn't read the page, we show "Check with airline" rather than guessing.

Disclaimer

Information may change at any time and may contain errors. It is provided for general guidance only and is not legal, veterinary or travel advice. Always confirm with the airline before booking or travelling, and check the government entry rules (e.g. CDC, USDA APHIS, CFIA, DEFRA) for every country on your route. We are not affiliated with any airline.

Affiliate disclosure

Some pages may contain affiliate links (for example to pet carriers or pet insurance). If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships never change the policy information we publish.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Product pages may link to Amazon.com through the Amazon Associates Program.

Advertising

The site shows ads served by Google AdSense and may load affiliate scripts such as Travelpayouts. Advertisers never change the policy information we publish. See our privacy policy for how advertising cookies work and how to opt out of personalized ads.

Corrections

Spotted something out of date? Please tell us via our contact page.
